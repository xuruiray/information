Lead Story
Moonshot AI Open-Sources 2.8 Trillion Parameter Kimi K3, Pushing Open-Source LLM Boundaries

Moonshot AI open-sourced its 2.8 trillion parameter Kimi K3 model on July 27, with weights totaling 1.56TB under a modified MIT license requiring attribution for large-scale use. This move significantly pushes the boundary of open-source LLMs in scale and capability, widely seen as a strong challenge to proprietary models.
