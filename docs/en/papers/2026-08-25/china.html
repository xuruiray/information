Policy & Regulation

The National People's Congress Standing Committee reviewed amendments to the Road Traffic Safety Law, covering autonomous driving regulations, penalties for distracted driving and road occupation groups, and new e-bike speed limits and helmet mandates. Meanwhile, the third China-US Track 1.5 Dialogue was held in Beijing, offering a channel for political trust-building and tech cooperation.

Draft Amendment to Traffic Law Specifies Penalties for Distracted Driving and Road Occupation Groups 中国新闻网时政

The draft amendment to China's Road Traffic Safety Law imposes fines of 200-500 yuan for distracted driving such as using phones or watching videos, with possible three-month license suspension. It also bans unauthorized road occupation by group activities like 'speed walking teams.'

The draft amendment raises the maximum speed for e-bikes on non-motorized lanes from 15 km/h to 20 km/h, prohibits speeding, wrong-way riding, running red lights and phone use, and mandates helmets for riders and passengers.

Over 50 Chinese and Foreign Trainees Gather in Yinchuan to Explore Arid Land Restoration 中国新闻网社会

The China-Central Asia Desertification Control Training Program opened in Yinchuan, with more than 50 officials, technical specialists and experts from Central Asian countries and China. The six-day course aims to share practices and explore cooperation in arid-region ecological restoration.

Read more → Chinese-Led Study Suggests 'Little Red Dots' May Reside in Dense Host Galaxies 中国新闻网社会

A study led by Chinese scholars, published in Nature Astronomy, suggests that the mysterious 'little red dots' in the universe may be located in small, compact host galaxies with a total mass of about one billion solar masses, offering new clues to understand these enigmatic objects.

Read more → Most Challenging Bridge on Yunnan Mengla-Lvchun Expressway Completed 中国新闻网社会

The main bridge of the Lixianjiang Grand Bridge, the most difficult section of the Mengla-Lvchun Expressway in Yunnan, has been joined, moving the project into its final phase. The expressway is scheduled to open by the end of 2026.
