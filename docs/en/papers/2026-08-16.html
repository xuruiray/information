Lead Story
Neptune’s tiny moons may be the wreckage of shattered ancient worlds

NASA's James Webb Space Telescope has uncovered surprising clues that Neptune's strange inner moons may be the wreckage of an ancient cosmic catastrophe. Researchers detected clay-like minerals on Larissa, Galatea, and Neptune's rings that form in the presence of liquid water, yet the moons themselves show no obvious water ice. The material likely came from deep inside much larger icy moons that were shattered long ago, reshaping our understanding of the Neptunian system.

Agreement Is Not Alignment: Divergent Moral Grounds in Human and LLM Ethical Judgments A new paper argues that agreement on final labels is a flawed proxy for alignment. Humans and LLMs may reach the same ethical judgment while relying on different moral grounds, so evaluating the reasoning process is essential to measure true alignment. The study calls for richer evaluations that look beyond aggregate agreement. arXiv 人工智能 Diagnostic Foundation for Evaluating LLMs' Research Integrity as Co-Scientists The paper introduces IntegrityBench, a benchmark that measures LLMs’ ability to classify misconduct, reason about ethical actions, and make evidence-grounded decisions under institutional pressure across 36 paired scenarios. It provides a diagnostic foundation for deploying LLMs as co-scientists while safeguarding research norms. The benchmark targets real-world tensions between productivity and integrity. arXiv 人工智能 Multi-Agent Scheduling with LLM-Assisted Contract Net Negotiation for Stream Processing in Mobile Edge Computing This work proposes MAS-DecStream, an LLM-assisted contract net negotiation framework for decentralized stream-processing scheduling across heterogeneous mobile edge–cloud systems, addressing workload volatility and QoS constraints. The approach aims to improve resource utilization and service guarantees in distributed environments, representing a step toward autonomous LLM-driven system management. arXiv 人工智能
