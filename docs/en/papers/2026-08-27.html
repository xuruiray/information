Lead Story
Nvidia agrees to acquire Hugging Face for $13B

Nvidia has agreed to acquire Hugging Face, the leading platform for hosting and sharing AI models, for $13 billion. The acquisition would bring a cornerstone of the open-source AI ecosystem under Nvidia's control, with far-reaching implications for model distribution, developer workflows, and competitive dynamics in the AI industry. The deal is expected to face regulatory scrutiny.
