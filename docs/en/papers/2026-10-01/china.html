Policy & Regulation Briefing

The Policy & Regulation section tracks recent government moves: the Ministry of Culture and Tourism released the 15th Five-Year Plan for arts development; the Beijing CPPCC proposed ways to develop modern rural industries around the capital; CAS researchers proposed alternating-current direct seawater electrolysis for offshore hydrogen production; and the Sino-Russian border rivers entered a 20-day autumn fishing ban.

Beijing CPPCC Offers Proposals on Modern Rural Industry Around the Capital 中国新闻网时政

According to China News Service, the Beijing Municipal CPPCC has put forward proposals on developing modern rural industries in the capital's suburban districts, offering policy reference for rural revitalisation and agricultural development around Beijing. The report was released on 30 September.

Read more → Ministry of Culture and Tourism Issues 15th Five-Year Plan for Arts Development 中国新闻网时政

The Ministry of Culture and Tourism has issued the 15th Five-Year Plan for Arts Development, following the fourth plenary session of the 20th CPC Central Committee and the national 15th Five-Year Plan outline, setting out arrangements for the arts in the coming period.

Read more → Chinese Team Proposes First Alternating-Current Direct Seawater Electrolysis Strategy for Hydrogen 中国新闻网时政

The Technical Institute of Physics and Chemistry under the Chinese Academy of Sciences said its national key laboratory has proposed, for the first time, using alternating current instead of direct current to electrolyse seawater directly for hydrogen, enabling offshore power-to-hydrogen and addressing precipitation bottlenecks.

Read more → Sino-Russian Border Rivers Enter 20-Day Autumn Fishing Ban, Over 700 Boats Leave Waters 中国新闻网时政

From midnight on 1 October, the Heilongjiang and Ussuri rivers on the China-Russia border entered a 20-day autumn fishing ban to protect cross-border chum salmon migration and breeding, with more than 700 fishing boats withdrawing. The two rivers have 55 ban days a year, split between summer and autumn.

Industry & Local Briefing

Industry & Local looks at regional economies and holiday consumption. The Three Gorges hub moved 120 million tonnes of cargo in the first three quarters, supporting the Yangtze waterway. Three Gorges Hub Cargo Volume Reaches 120 Million Tonnes in First Three Quarters of 2026 中国新闻网社会

The Three Gorges Navigation Administration said the hub's total throughput reached 120 million tonnes in the first three quarters of 2026, continuing to underpin the Yangtze golden waterway and economic development in riverside areas. The figure was released on 1 October.

Read more → Surfing Brings New Rural Businesses to a Fishing Village in Cangnan, Zhejiang 中国新闻网社会

China News Service reports that a small fishing village in Cangnan, Wenzhou, Zhejiang, has found new life through surfing, with the sport driving fresh rural momentum and offering an example of rural revitalisation combined with sports tourism. The report was published on 1 October.

Read more → Yangtze Delta Rail Catering Upgraded for National Day Holiday with Autumn Menus 中国新闻网社会

As Yangtze River Delta railways saw a National Day travel peak on 1 October, Huatie Catering under China Railway Shanghai Group launched new "A Good Bowl of Rice" meal options to upgrade onboard dining, described as autumn health-oriented dishes for travellers.

Read more → Sanya Tightens Tourism Consumer-Protection Network to Turn Complaints into Praise 中国新闻网社会

China News Service reports that Sanya is strengthening its tourism consumption environment across dining, lodging, transport, shopping and entertainment, using measures such as seafood scales and out-of-island complaint channels to turn critical ratings into praise. The city promotes the effort as a reassurance network for visitors.

Society & Law Briefing

Society & Law covers enforcement and daily life: Zhejiang's coast guard seized more than 150 tonnes of smuggled frozen food off Xiangshan, worth over 10 million yuan; rescuers from Conghua, Guangzhou, pulled occupants from a burning car on a highway; over 101,000 people watched the flag-raising at Tiananmen Square marking the 77th anniversary of the PRC; and a flash-mob celebration was held aboard a Beijing-Shanghai high-speed train.

A "Fishing Boat" Exposes a Smuggling Case Worth Over 10 Million Yuan 中国新闻网社会

Zhejiang's coast guard said on 1 October that a joint operation by Ningbo coast guard, police, border inspection and fishery enforcement seized a smuggling vessel off Xiangshan, detaining four suspects and confiscating over 150 tonnes of frozen goods including beef tendon, with the case valued at more than 10 million yuan.

Read more → Rescue Team Members in Conghua, Guangzhou Save Trapped Occupants from Burning Car on Highway 中国新闻网社会

The Feixiang Emergency Rescue Association in Conghua, Guangzhou, said seven members returning from a diving-training course encountered a car on fire on the Beijing-Zhuhai expressway near Zhongluotan, with a passenger trapped, and carried out a rapid, methodical rescue.

Read more → Over 100,000 Gather at Tiananmen Square for Flag-Raising on PRC's 77th Anniversary 中国新闻网社会

A flag-raising ceremony was held at Tiananmen Square in Beijing on 1 October, with more than 101,000 people from across the country gathering to mark the 77th anniversary of the founding of the People's Republic of China, China News Service reported.

Read more → National Day Flash-Mob Event Staged Aboard Beijing-Shanghai High-Speed Train 中国新闻网社会

On the evening of 30 September, train G30 operated by the Shanghai passenger section of China Railway Shanghai Group departed Shanghai Hongqiao station, where a National Day-themed flash-mob event was held in carriage No. 9 decorated for the holiday.
