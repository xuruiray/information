Space, Energy & Robotics Briefing

This subsection covers quantum measurement, AI video detection and Earth observation: a light-powered AI identifies deepfakes with nearly 98% accuracy; Caltech used laser-trapped atoms to recreate quantum tipping points and measure an energy ladder predicted 40 years ago; NASA imagery captured record flooding along the Gandak River in Nepal and India.

This light-powered AI can spot deepfakes with nearly 98% accuracy ScienceDaily 技术

UCLA researchers have built a light-powered AI system that analyzes more than a dozen videos simultaneously and detects deepfakes with nearly 98% accuracy. The system is fast, energy-efficient and resistant to adversarial attacks, making it a promising tool for screening the growing flood of AI-generated video.

Read more → Caltech physicists finally measure a quantum energy ladder predicted 40 years ago ScienceDaily 技术

By arranging laser-trapped atoms into a quantum simulator, researchers recreated two different quantum tipping points and watched the atoms settle into the exact energy ratios predicted by theory. The experiment confirms a prediction made about 40 years ago and could be used to explore other quantum systems.

Read more → Rains Swamp the Gandak River NASA 技术

After days of torrential rain, the Gandak River rose to record-breaking levels in parts of Nepal and India. NASA’s Earth Observatory released satellite imagery showing the flooded river. The images help document the extent of the floodwaters across the region, which affected communities along the river.

Scientific Research Briefing

This subsection features quantum computing, neurodegenerative disease and nutrition science: a superfluid helium qubit design could cut error rates roughly 100-fold; reversible damage from the APOE4 gene offers new Alzheimer’s targets; the MIND diet is linked to about 2.5 years of delayed brain aging; and a new “Plant Armor” fabric more than triples strawberry yields.

This new qubit could be 100 times less error-prone in superfluid quantum computer breakthrough ScienceDaily 科学

A proposed qubit made with superfluid helium could cut quantum computing error rates by around 100 times by shielding quantum information from common electromagnetic noise. If experiments confirm the predictions, the technology could eventually work alongside today’s superconducting qubits or serve as a new kind of quantum memory.

Read more → Alzheimer’s risk gene APOE4 may have a reversible weakness ScienceDaily 科学

The Alzheimer’s risk gene APOE4 may actively damage brain blood vessels and sabotage the cellular systems that remove harmful proteins. Researchers were able to reverse some of these effects in experiments, revealing promising new targets for Alzheimer’s, Parkinson’s and other neurodegenerative diseases.

Read more → This diet may slow brain aging by 2.5 years ScienceDaily 科学

People who followed the MIND diet more closely showed slower brain shrinkage and less grey matter loss over time, with the strongest differences equivalent to about 2.5 years of delayed brain aging. Berries and poultry were linked with healthier brain changes, while sweets and fried fast foods were associated with greater tissue loss.

Read more → New “plant armor” more than triples strawberry yields ScienceDaily 科学

A new protective fabric boosted strawberry yields by as much as 3.56 times while blocking insects and still allowing sunlight, rain and airflow to reach the plants. Originally inspired by research on military clothing, Plant Armor could also help farms reduce pesticide and water use.
