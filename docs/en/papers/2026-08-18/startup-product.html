New Products & Growth Briefing

This subsection covers three notable product launches. TinyFish introduces a web operating layer for AI agents, aiming to standardize agent interactions with online services. Hansel by Seedling provides a self-hosted email solution that keeps server control and encryption keys in users' hands, addressing vendor lock-in concerns. Vendo empowers end users to build their own features inside a product, turning software into a platform for community-driven customization. Together, these products signal growing momentum in AI infrastructure, digital sovereignty, and user-centric product design.

TinyFish Product Hunt

TinyFish positions itself as the web operating layer for AI agents, providing a unified environment for agents to navigate, interact, and execute tasks across websites. By abstracting common web operations, it aims to lower the barrier for AI agents to work with complex web interfaces and drive broader adoption of agent-driven workflows.

Read more → Vendo Product Hunt

Vendo enables end users to build and assemble custom features directly inside a product without relying on the development team, turning software into an extensible platform that users can shape themselves. This approach encourages community-driven customization and reduces the backlog of feature requests. It reflects a shift toward user-centric product design where customers participate actively in product evolution.
