China Watch: Legal Support for Green Transition, Multiple Social Improvements

This edition of China Watch covers policy and social developments. On the policy front, Guangdong High Court issued 15 measures to support green and low-carbon growth; Yangquan, Shanxi, is transforming from an energy base to a computing hub; Wenchang, Hainan, unveiled policies to boost the aerospace industry; and the Ministry of Public Security released 14 typical cases of online rumors against enterprises. On the social and legal side, viral overseas trends are fueling a China travel boom; a college student faces a lawsuit over a deposit-free camera rental, highlighting new consumer risks; 'silver-age' personal trainers are bringing home fitness to the elderly; and medical insurance payment reform is making community healthcare more affordable. Together, these stories show China's governance and social fabric evolving with new challenges and opportunities.
