AI & LLMs Briefing

This edition of AI & LLMs focuses on frontier models and research governance. On the frontier, MobileMem explores long-term memory for AI agents; new research finds that sample-level regressions under model version updates are hard to predict, and warns that benchmark-specific optimization does not imply general coding ability. Jais 2 introduces an Arabic-centric family of open LLMs. Governance coverage includes layer sensitivity in Mixture-of-Experts models, workload evolution in LLM serving, and new policy ideas for the Intelligence Age. The overall picture: the industry is shifting from raw capability races toward reliability, cost efficiency, and governance.

Frontier Models Briefing

Frontier models coverage spans long-term memory for agents, sample-level regression after version updates, token inflation routing for agentic systems, the Jais 2 Arabic-centric open models, and a critique of benchmark-oriented optimization. MobileMem proposes learning persistently from user experiences; research shows version updates can silently break responses for individual samples; Jais 2 strengthens Arabic language modeling; and a new paper argues benchmark tuning does not guarantee broader coding capability.

MobileMem: Learning from a Year of Mobile Experiences arXiv 人工智能

Next-generation AI agents are moving toward persistent personal assistants that can understand, remember, and learn from users' experiences. MobileMem builds on a year of mobile experiences to study the long-term memory mechanisms these agents need to accumulate and apply user-specific knowledge.

Read more → No Universal Signal Predicts Sample-Level LLM Regression under Version Updates arXiv 人工智能

Frontier LLMs are updated frequently and often improve in aggregate, but individual samples can regress from correct to incorrect. The paper finds no universal signal reliably predicts such sample-level regression under version updates, urging evaluation beyond aggregate metrics.

Read more → Not All Tokens Are Equal: Inflation-Aware Routing for Agentic LLM Systems arXiv 计算语言学

When a language model fails on the first try, agentic systems retry and burn extra tokens, creating a gap between per-token price and real workflow cost. The paper defines this as token inflation and proposes an inflation-aware routing mechanism to control spending.

Read more → Jais 2: A Family of Arabic-Centric Open Large Language Models arXiv 计算语言学

Jais 2, developed by MBZUAI, Cerebras, and Inception, is an Arabic-centric family of open large language models. It shows strong results on Arabic and culturally grounded benchmarks, advancing Arabic language modeling and localized AI.

Read more → Don't Claim Benchmark-Oriented Optimization Improves General Coding Capability -- Diverse Evaluation Is Required arXiv 机器学习

Many post-training papers and model cards treat a few coding benchmarks, such as SWE-bench and LiveCodeBench, as evidence of general coding capability. The new paper argues that benchmark-oriented optimization only boosts task-specific scores and calls for diverse evaluation.

Research & Governance Briefing

Research & governance coverage includes layer-wise sensitivity analysis for Mixture-of-Experts compression, a year-long study of LLM serving workloads and caching, L-FNO for stochastic event dynamics, and robustness improvements to RVFL networks. On policy, OpenAI is funding 14 independent projects exploring ways to expand economic opportunity and resilience in the Intelligence Age.

A Year in LLM Serving: Workload Evolution, Caching and Load-Balancing arXiv 人工智能

LLM serving has become a critical cloud workload, yet existing studies are limited in scale and duration. This paper uses a year of data to characterize workload evolution, caching patterns, and load-balancing demands, offering realistic scenarios for system design and benchmarking.

Read more → Depth-Aware Sensitivity Analysis of Mixture-of-Experts Models via Magnitude-Based Expert Masking arXiv 人工智能

Mixture-of-Experts architectures scale LLMs through sparse activation, yet the relative importance of individual MoE layers is poorly understood. The paper introduces magnitude-based expert masking for depth-aware sensitivity analysis, supporting compression and optimization.

Read more → L-FNO: Lorentzian Fourier Neural Operator for Stochastic Event Dynamics arXiv 机器学习

Operational systems face rare, bursty, self-exciting events even in routine conditions, driven by external covariates and internal dynamics. L-FNO incorporates Lorentzian structure into Fourier neural operators to estimate conditional intensity and better predict stochastic events.

Read more → Robust Dual-Model Collaborative Random Vector Functional Link Network arXiv 机器学习

Random vector functional link (RVFL) networks are lightweight and fast but sensitive to noisy labels, outliers, and imbalanced data. A dual-model collaborative mechanism improves robustness while keeping the efficiency, broadening edge-use applicability.
