TAO Responds to Taiwan-Related Report and US Analysis, Discusses Rights Protection; He Lifeng Meets French Advisor

On Oct 14, the Taiwan Affairs Office responded to a European Parliament report on Taiwan that urged rejecting China's pressure on international organizations and limiting Taiwan's international exchanges, and said the EU's "One China policy" does not endorse the One China principle. It also responded to a Reuters report citing a US official's analysis that the likelihood of a mainland attack on Taiwan before 2028 has decreased; Taiwan's DPP authorities said the mainland's intent to "annex" Taiwan by force has never changed. The TAO said it protects Taiwanese compatriots' lawful rights and interests in accordance with law and attaches importance to resolving Taiwan-related investment disputes, with many Taiwanese businessmen sending thanks. On Oct 13, He Lifeng met French President's diplomatic advisor Bonne at the Great Hall of the People, exchanging views on China-France practical economic and trade cooperation and China-EU economic and trade relations.

SPC Releases Plant Variety Protection Cases; Rare Birds Found at Jiugong Mountain; CMA Activates Level IV Rainstorm Response

On Oct 14, the Supreme People's Court released typical cases on plant variety protection: a company was ordered to pay over 7.6 million yuan for unauthorized breeding and sale of new passion fruit seedlings; a base that stole branches to grow and sell unauthorized blueberry seedlings was subject to punitive damages. During an autumn bird survey, Hubei's Jiugong Mountain National Nature Reserve recorded the critically endangered yellow-breasted bunting, a national first-class protected animal, for the first time, along with the black morph of the brown shrike, black kite, and other rare birds. The China Meteorological Administration activated a Level IV emergency response for major meteorological disasters (rainstorms) at 10:00 on Oct 14, with heavy to torrential rain expected from Oct 14 to 15 in parts of southeastern Chongqing, northeastern Guizhou, southern Hubei, northern Hunan, and northern Jiangxi.
