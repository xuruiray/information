Lead Story
Fed rate hike fails to calm troubled markets as Dow falls 600 points. Expect more sharp swings in stocks and bonds.

The Federal Reserve raised rates for the first time since 2023, with Chair Kevin Warsh signaling resolve to fight inflation. The move failed to calm markets: the Dow fell 600 points and both stocks and bonds swung sharply, with the article warning of more volatility ahead. The hike also pressured Hong Kong property stocks and Asian currencies, with the yen in focus before the Bank of Japan's Friday policy meeting.
