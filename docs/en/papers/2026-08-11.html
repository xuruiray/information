Lead Story
Introducing Muse Glimmer

Meta is back in the open-weights game with Muse Glimmer, a brand new 30B model released under a clean Apache 2.0 license. The model is optimized for end-to-end agentic task completion and achieves a strong success rate in local use. The permissive license is a step up from Meta’s earlier Llama licenses and is expected to accelerate adoption in open-source and on-premises deployments.
