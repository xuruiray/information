Lead Story
OpenRouter is joining Stripe

OpenRouter, a platform providing unified API access to various AI models, announced it is joining Stripe. Reports had indicated Stripe would acquire OpenRouter for over $7 billion. The integration is expected to transform AI model distribution and billing, tying developer tools more closely with payment infrastructure. The Hacker News community responded with extensive discussion. This move reflects the growing commercialization of AI infrastructure and consolidates the AI model gateway market under a major fintech player.

GxP-Agent: Process-DAG Topology for Reliable Clinical Trial Programming with LLM Agents Clinical trial programming transforms study protocols into CDISC-compliant analysis-ready datasets, a critical bottleneck in regulatory submissions. Across eleven single-shot attempts with five frontier models, none produces a valid subject-level dataset. GxP-Agent addresses this by introducing a process-DAG topology that guides LLM agents through the required workflow, markedly improving reliability and output validity in a high-stakes medical software setting. arXiv 人工智能 DiSCO: Defending text-to-image generation through distribution-guided contrastive prompt optimization Progress in text-to-image generation raises serious safety concerns, especially the production of NSFW content such as violence and nudity, which red-team adversarial attacks can further amplify. Many existing defenses rely on white-box assumptions and access to model internals. DiSCO offers a distribution-guided contrastive prompt optimization method that strengthens the robustness of text-to-image systems against adversarial prompts while preserving generation quality, contributing a practical defense for safer generative AI. arXiv 人工智能 Institution-Specific LLM Prompting Recovers PHI That De-identification Systems and Their Gold Standards Both Miss Secondary use of electronic health records requires robust de-identification, yet standard systems often miss institutionally situated protected health information, such as hospital abbreviations, building names, and internal codes whose sensitivity is locally determined. This study demonstrates that institution-specific LLM prompting can recover PHI overlooked by both contemporary de-identification systems and their gold-standard test sets. The findings suggest a complementary role for large language models in privacy-preserving health-data pipelines, with direct implications for compliance and data governance. arXiv 计算语言学
