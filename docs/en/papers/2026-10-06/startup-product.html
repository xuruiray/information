New Products & Growth

This week's new products center on agent-native tools. HyperFrames Studio launched a video editor built for agents; crosswalk offers a shared inbox-based space for people and their agents; Jarq translates, shortens, fixes, or rewrites text near the cursor; and Cloudflare's Web Search API gives AI agents access to live internet data. Together they target creation, collaboration, and information retrieval in agent workflows.

Jarq Product Hunt

Jarq is a lightweight writing assistant that translates, shortens, fixes, or rewrites any text near the cursor. It aims to reduce switching between applications and manual editing steps, joining a wave of tools that embed AI directly into everyday writing surfaces.
