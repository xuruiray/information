National Climate Center: Super El Nino Has Officially Formed 中国新闻网社会

China's National Climate Center said on October 9 that a super El Nino has officially formed. The equatorial central and eastern Pacific entered El Nino conditions in May 2026, and the July–September sea surface temperature sliding average exceeded 2.5°C, meeting the threshold for a super event of the eastern-Pacific type.

Heavy rainfall brought torrential downpours to parts of Hainan, prompting red rainstorm alerts and school suspensions in some areas. Meteorological authorities warned of risks including urban waterlogging, flash floods and geological disasters.
