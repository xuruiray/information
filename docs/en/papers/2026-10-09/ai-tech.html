Frontier Models

Frontier Models Briefing

The Frontier Models subsection covers agentic planning, tool use, data synthesis, and efficiency. Plan-and-Patch applies diffusion language models to planning; StoreBench offers a live-commerce environment for operator agents; simulation-based synthesis generates enterprise data; and long-context memory and compressed text representation research aims to improve model efficiency.

Plan-and-Patch: Diffusion Language Models for Agentic Planning arXiv 人工智能

The paper introduces Plan-and-Patch, applying diffusion language models to long-horizon agentic planning. It aims to handle invalidated assumptions, unexpected tool outputs, and failed actions, improving subgoal coordination and execution robustness in multi-step tasks.

Read more → Synthesis Through Simulation: Generating Coherent Enterprise Data via Scalable Agent-System Interaction arXiv 人工智能

The paper addresses constraints on training and evaluating tool-calling agents caused by restrictions on enterprise systems, data, and database schemas. It proposes simulation through scalable agent-system interaction to generate coherent enterprise tabular data for training and evaluation.

Read more → StoreBench: A Live-Commerce Environment for Evaluating and Training Autonomous Operator Agents arXiv 人工智能

The paper notes that most agentic benchmarks remain static, with terminal rewards and arbitrary pass bars. StoreBench introduces a live-commerce environment for evaluating and training autonomous operator agents, where the world changes as the agent acts and reward and pass criteria are redesigned.

Read more → Lossy Compressive Text Autoencoders arXiv 计算语言学

The work explores learned compressed latent representations of text at the intersection of data compression and representation learning. The authors propose an autoencoder architecture that performs residual downscaling and upscaling of hidden representations along the time axis, with a low-dimensional discrete representation to learn compressed text.

Read more → Real Long-Term Memory for AI: A 50-Million-Token Window That Is Faster and Cheaper Than Recompute arXiv 计算语言学

The paper tests a memory layer, the public package galahad-kv, which saves the KV state of each block of about 16,000 tokens to encrypted local NVM. This avoids recomputing internal KV state for every prompt and supports a 50-million-token window, which the authors say is faster and cheaper than recompute.

Research & Governance

5 articles
Research & Governance Briefing

The Research & Governance subsection covers normative competence in LLMs, compliance-bounded self-evolution, LLM-assisted transportation management plans, semantic table interpretation, and behavior cloning. The selected papers examine alignment, compliance, explainability, and long-horizon learning, asking how AI systems can become more reviewable and reliable.

Reading the Room: Foundations, Design, and Challenges of Normative Competence in LLMs arXiv 人工智能

The paper discusses how human communities are governed by normative systems and how aligning increasingly autonomous AI systems with these norms is a central challenge. It examines foundations, design, and challenges of normative competence in LLMs, including the vast and open-ended nature of norms.

Read more → The Harness as the Only Mutable Surface: Compliance-Bounded Self-Evolution of LLM Agents in Credit Pipelines, with a Measured Admission Gate arXiv 人工智能

The paper argues that self-improving LLM agents in credit pipelines can adapt to changed rules, but an agent that rewrites itself destroys reviewable artifacts such as named changes, recorded tests, and approvals. It proposes confining self-evolution to the runtime harness and adding a measured admission gate.

Read more → Large Language Model-Assisted Preparation of Transportation Management Plans: A Case Study with WisDOT WisTMP System arXiv 计算语言学

The paper addresses labor-intensive transportation management plan preparation, which relies heavily on practitioner expertise. Using the Wisconsin DOT WisTMP system as a case study, it proposes large language model assistance to help prepare plans and improve safety and mobility in work zones.

Read more → An Explainable Header-Centric Framework for Large-Scale Semantic Table Interpretation and Data Quality Assessment arXiv 人工智能

The paper notes that knowledge graph quality depends on tabular metadata quality before integration. In metadata-only semantic table interpretation, where cell values are unavailable, noisy, or unsuitable, column headers become a critical source of semantic evidence. It proposes an explainable header-centric framework for interpretation and data quality assessment.

Read more → Self-Supervised Keyframe Discovery for Horizon-Invariant Behavior Cloning arXiv 人工智能

The paper examines behavior cloning in non-Markovian environments, where policies must reason over long-horizon context. Existing recurrent or attention-based architectures face limitations. It proposes self-supervised keyframe discovery for horizon-invariant behavior cloning to better capture long-term dependencies.
