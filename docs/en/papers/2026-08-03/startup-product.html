VC-Backed Startups Commit More Fraud, Researchers Think They Know Why TechCrunch 创业

New research from Imperial College London and Emlyon Business School maps how founders of venture capital-backed startups commit fraud and examines the role investors play in these dynamics. The findings suggest that the pressure of rapid growth and fundraising may increase the likelihood of fraudulent behavior. The study offers a detailed look at the mechanisms behind such misconduct and raises questions about accountability within the VC ecosystem.
