China Watch: Official Incentives, Wage Arrears Governance and E-Bike Regulation in Focus

On November 27, Chongqing set out measures to improve incentives for officials to take responsibility; the Supreme People's Court released six typical cases on wage arrears; and the State Administration for Market Regulation summoned 15 electric bicycle companies, including Yadea, Tailg, Aima, Xinri, Ninebot and Niu, for collective talks. Also in focus were the Yangtze River Economic Belt's innovation and IP progress, Chinese robot makers going global, Jiashi's Newmei plum poverty reduction case, and three new high-speed rail lines in Anhui. Zhang Feng, chairman of AOPA China, is under disciplinary review and investigation; Li Shutian, former deputy director of Henan's market regulation bureau, was expelled from the Party and public office; Ningbo expanded its airborne medical rescue network; and the Global Health and Education Foundation will donate rare animal specimens and pilot a rural nature education program in Chongqing.
