Science and Tech: Tiny UV Chip, Primordial Black Hole Lead, and Life-Science Findings

This Science and Technology section covers chips and hardware, space, energy and robotics, and scientific research. A Harvard-led team built a microscopic device that converts red light into ultraviolet light with about 120 times the output of comparable prior methods; astronomers detected a candidate primordial black hole named Phoebe, possibly formed soon after the Big Bang and about three lunar masses; and genetic data spanning more than a decade for five deep-sea fish and shrimp species in the Gulf of Mexico showed extinction-risk signals in every species examined. Other studies challenge the view that neurons are highly specialized, identify two potentially critical ages for children’s screen exposure, and reclassify a 345-million-year-old fossil as an aquatic or amphibious creature using X-ray imaging.
