Seven Killed in Guizhou Longli Traffic Accident; Initial Probe Points to Drunk Driving and Overloading 中国新闻网社会

A traffic accident in Longli County, Guizhou Province, early on Sept. 29 killed seven people. An initial investigation found the driver was drunk and the vehicle was overloaded. Local authorities activated an emergency response and dispatched emergency and medical teams.

Read more → Hainan to Strengthen Three Defense Lines for Flood and Drought Prevention in 15th Five-Year Plan 中国新闻网社会

Hainan will accelerate its rainfall and hydrological monitoring and forecasting system, focusing on three defense lines during the 15th Five-Year Plan period, said Jia Changqing, deputy director of the provincial water resources department. The aim is to extend flood lead times and improve forecast accuracy.

Employers may ask new hires to sign labor contracts, waivers of social insurance, or even resignation agreements. Using three typical labor dispute cases, the article explains the legal consequences of signing such documents at the onboarding stage and urges workers to review them carefully.
