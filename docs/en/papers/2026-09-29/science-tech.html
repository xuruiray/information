Blood tests reveal why astronauts get constipated in space ScienceDaily 科学

Spaceflight may dramatically alter the gut within weeks, with bacteria breaking down more protein as food moves more slowly through the intestines. The shift could help explain astronaut constipation and may have wider effects on health, making it an important concern for future missions to Mars.

Measurements of ancient light from the cosmic microwave background suggest the universe is remarkably flat. But that does not prove it is infinite: the observable cosmos could be only a small patch of a much larger curved universe, and space could even be flat while wrapping around on itself in exotic ways.
