Science and Technology: Lunar Reactor, Early Black Holes and Memory Therapy

NASA is seeking industry partners to develop and test Lunar Reactor-1, a lunar fission system intended to generate 20 kilowatts of electricity. MIT scientists propose that atom-sized primordial black holes from the Big Bang could consume stars from within and trigger visible explosions. A massive rotating structure near the galactic center produces shock waves that trigger star formation, while part of the Norma spiral arm may not be a spiral arm at all. Astronomers found the oldest known compact quasar pair, existing 900 million years after the Big Bang, with black hole masses of about 1.5 billion and 400 million suns. In medicine, an mRNA-vaccine-like nasal treatment restored memory in aging mice and prevented severe cognitive decline in Alzheimer’s-like mice. Ancient far-side lunar rocks suggest asteroid impacts declined gradually over billions of years. Plants beneath the Barnes Ice Cap in the Canadian Arctic suggest the next ice age may have begun about 2,000 years ago before global warming stopped it. Newly discovered viruses may reveal a missing link in the early evolution of biological structures.
