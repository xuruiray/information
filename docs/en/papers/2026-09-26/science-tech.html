Quantum Computing’s ‘Dark Horse’ Just Proved It Can Go Universal ScienceDaily 技术

Researchers have shown that non-Abelian anyons can perform the full range of operations needed for universal quantum computing. Using 54 qubits on Quantinuum’s H2 processor, they combined braiding and fusion to unlock capabilities that braiding alone could not provide. The result suggests a path toward more robust quantum computers.
