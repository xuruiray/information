Jango Product Hunt

Jango is a testing tool for multi-user applications that uses AI agents to act like real users. It helps developers uncover issues before launch, aiming to lower the cost of testing multi-user scenarios and help teams validate product experiences faster. The product targets teams that need to simulate real-user behavior in complex apps.

Basedash MCP write brings data visualization into Cursor and Claude, letting users create charts and dashboards directly in their development environment. The feature targets technical teams that want to stay in their coding interface.
