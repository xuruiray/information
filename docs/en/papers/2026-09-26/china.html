Third Xinjiang Kashgar Cultural Industry Expo and Trade Fair Opens 中国新闻网时政

The third Xinjiang Kashgar Cultural Industry Expo and Trade Fair, themed Bright Moon over the Silk Road, Cultural Expo Boosts Kashgar, opened in Kashgar on September 25. Running for three days, it is currently the only cultural exhibition in Xinjiang approved and retained by the Ministry of Commerce, focusing on cultural industry display and trade.

Read more → Adjustment to Advance Booking Window for Pilot Train Ticket Reservation Service; Passengers Should Note 中国新闻网时政

Starting September 26, the advance booking window for the pilot train ticket reservation service on the Beijing–Shanghai high-speed and conventional railways will gradually shift from 60–17 days before departure to 30–17 days before departure. A transition period runs from September 27 to October 26, with the new rule fully in effect from October 27.
