Lead Story
NASA's Webb finds signs of Mars-sized worlds smashing together

NASA's James Webb Space Telescope has observed 21 rare "extreme debris disks" around young stars, finding dust that reveals the scale and violence of planetary collisions. Researchers say these smash-ups may resemble the giant impact thought to have created Earth's Moon, offering clues to how our own solar system formed and evolved.
