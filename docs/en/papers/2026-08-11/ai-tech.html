Learning to Predict Middle-Layer Attention in MLLMs for Visual Token Pruning arXiv 人工智能

Multimodal large language models are strong on vision-language tasks but slowed by processing many visual tokens. Visual token pruning cuts cost yet requires accurate importance estimates. This paper proposes predicting middle-layer attention to better locate prunable tokens, improving inference efficiency without sacrificing performance.

Read more → Do AI Personas Grow? Analyzing and Benchmarking Personality Evolution in LLM Agents After Life Events arXiv 计算语言学

Personality-conditioned LLM agents are increasingly used in emotional support, social simulation, and role-playing, driving interest in lifelong agents. Personality evolution is key to coherence: agents should change plausibly after life events. This work systematically analyzes and benchmarks personality evolution in LLM agents.

ED-CSP: Crystal Structure Prediction from Electron Diffraction arXiv 机器学习

Recovering periodic 3D crystal structures from sparse, unindexed electron diffraction data is a challenging generative inverse problem. Existing methods mainly predict labels or reconstruct from indexed reflections. ED-CSP proposes a new generative approach, broadening AI-driven materials structure prediction.
