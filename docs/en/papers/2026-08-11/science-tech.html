Scientists Discover a Hidden Switch Inside Silver Nanocatalysts ScienceDaily 科学

Silver nanocatalysts switch where they drive key reactions depending on whether a solid oxide cell is producing electricity or hydrogen. The discovery could enable smarter catalyst designs for cleaner power and more efficient green hydrogen production.

Read more → Cassini Reveals a Surprising Twist in Saturn’s Magnetic Shield ScienceDaily 科学

Cassini data shows Saturn's rapid rotation drags a key magnetosphere opening far toward the afternoon side, unlike Earth's near-noon position. The finding confirms that fast-spinning giant planets operate under a fundamentally different magnetic regime.

The small predator's unusual leg positioning may have made it more efficient than earlier reptiles; its relatives were previously known mainly on other continents.
