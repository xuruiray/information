Lead Story
OpenAI will start watermarking ChatGPT’s text in the EU

OpenAI will begin watermarking text generated by ChatGPT and Codex in the EU to comply with the AI Act. The watermarks are invisible, but editing can make them harder to detect, the company says. Detection tools will initially be available to researchers. OpenAI has outlined its approach to text provenance under EU rules, explaining where watermarks apply and how detection works. The move marks a practical step in AI content provenance regulation as the EU enforces its AI Act.
