AI & LLMs Briefing

This edition of AI & LLMs follows two threads. Research & Governance

5 articles
Research & Governance Briefing

The research and governance subsection centres on evaluation and trust. It audits blind spots in deep imbalanced regression evaluation, automates verification of peer-review claims, and introduces 68 open Erdos problems in Lean as a reasoning benchmark. Related work tests numerical representation invariance and mitigates LLM over-refusal under safety alignment.

Exposing Blind Spots in Deep Imbalanced Regression Evaluation arXiv 机器学习

The paper examines deep imbalanced regression, where highly non-uniform target distributions let models excel in dense regions while failing elsewhere. It audits current evaluation practice, arguing that existing metrics obscure performance on rare, sparsely populated targets.

Read more → Same Quantity, Different Answer: Numerical Representation Invariance in Language Models arXiv 计算语言学

The authors generate 3,600 exact-rational problems and 8,600 prompts spanning five identity-preserving transformations, including decimals, fractions, percentages, number words, scientific notation and converted units, to test whether models return consistent canonical answers.

Read more → Mitigating LLM Over-Refusal via Dynamic Semantic Routing Calibration arXiv 计算语言学

The paper studies over-refusal, in which safety-aligned LLMs wrongly reject benign yet safety-related instructions. Departing from the usual static representation-overlap explanation, the authors analyse the underlying dynamics and propose dynamic semantic routing calibration to mitigate it.

Read more → Peerify: Benchmarking Peer-Review Claim Verification arXiv 计算语言学

The authors present Peerify, a pipeline for manuscript-grounded verification of claims made in peer review, addressing a process that remains largely manual and time-consuming in scholarly publishing.

Read more → FrontierMath Erdos: A Benchmark of 68 Open Conjectures arXiv 计算语言学

The paper introduces FrontierMath Erdos (FME), a benchmark of 68 Erdos problems that remain open as of August 2026. To solve a task, AI systems must prove or disprove one of the conjectures in the Lean proof assistant.
