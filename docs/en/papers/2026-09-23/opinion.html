SAML: A fractal of bad design Hacker News

A post on the Trail of Bits blog critiques SAML, the widely used single sign-on protocol, arguing that its design flaws recur at multiple levels and make implementation error-prone. The Hacker News discussion drew 190 points and 117 comments, reflecting strong interest from developers who work with identity systems. The article's title calls SAML 'a fractal of bad design,' and the conversation focuses on complexity, specification issues, and practical security implications.

Obscura: VPN that can't log your activity Hacker News

Obscura launched a VPN service that claims it cannot log user activity, positioning privacy as its core technical promise. The Hacker News post drew 102 points and 84 comments, with community members debating whether the no-log claim can be trusted and how such a system might be audited. The discussion touches on privacy tools, trust models, and the practical limits of VPN security. It reflects ongoing community interest in services that reduce data collection.
