Railway holiday transport begins; 284 million passenger trips expected 中国新闻网社会

China's railway transport for the Mid-Autumn Festival and National Day began on Sept 23 and runs 16 days through Oct 8. The national network expects 284 million passenger trips, with about 13,000 trains scheduled daily, and the peak is expected on Oct 1.

Policies, events and services are being used to support holiday spending so that residents gain, businesses draw more customers and market confidence improves.

In Shuibei village, Daba town, Heyuan city, Guangdong province, the golden passion fruit harvest is under way. Fruit picked from the trellises is graded and packed at a sorting centre before shipping, with the crop now a key source of income for local households.

Cardiovascular disease remains the leading cause of death among Chinese residents.
