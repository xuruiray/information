Opinion & Analysis: AI Reshapes Work and Thought

This edition of Opinion & Analysis focuses on how AI is reshaping technical work and intellectual ambition. A developer documents using Codex for auto-research to iteratively optimize a kernel, achieving a 232x performance gain. Another post argues that collaborating with AI feels more like leadership than coding—key tasks are setting goals, evaluating outputs and guiding direction. A further essay cautions against overstating AI's cognitive abilities, noting that AI can draw on vastly larger working memory than the human brain, but that this does not mean AI is outthinking mathematicians. Together, these pieces capture the tension between AI enthusiasm and careful assessment. They have provoked hundreds of comments on topics from prompt-driven research to mathematical creativity, and offer a snapshot of how practitioners are integrating—and questioning—AI's expanding role in professional life.
