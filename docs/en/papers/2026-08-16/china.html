5.2-Magnitude Earthquake Strikes Waters East of Taiwan 中国新闻网时政

A 5.2-magnitude earthquake struck waters east of Taiwan at 7:30 PM on August 15, according to Taiwan's meteorological agency. The quake was felt strongly in Yilan, Hualien and New Taipei, as well as multiple other locations along the eastern and northern parts of the island.

Read more → Film 'Love Letters to Grandma' Premieres in Taiwan 中国新闻网时政

Drawing a large crowd, the film 'Love Letters to Grandma' premiered in Taiwan at the 2026 Taoyuan Film Festival, screened in the Special Screening section on August 15 and 16. Long queues formed before the screening, and many audience members left the theater with teary eyes.

Read more → 2026 Cross-Strait Youth Baseball and Softball Exchange Opens in Nanchang 中国新闻网时政

The 2026 Cross-Strait Youth Baseball and Softball Exchange kicked off on August 15 in Nanchang, Jiangxi Province, gathering nearly 130 athletes from Jiangxi and Taiwan for friendly competition. The event aims to foster youth exchanges, mutual understanding and friendship across the strait through sports, as part of broader cross-strait people-to-people exchanges.

Read more → 2026 Cross-Strait Youth Science and Technology Education Exchange Opens in Fuzhou 中国新闻网时政

The 2026 Cross-Strait Youth Science and Technology Education Exchange opened on August 15 in Fuzhou, intending to strengthen mutual learning and cooperation between young people across the strait in science, technology and education fields, thereby deepening educational ties. It is part of ongoing efforts to promote cross-strait civil exchanges.
