Science and Technology: Primordial Black Hole Candidate and Deep Red Sea Brine Pools

This edition covers two discoveries: astronomers detected a mysterious object named Phoebe when it briefly magnified a distant star; it may be a primordial black hole formed soon after the Big Bang, with about three times the Moon’s mass, and could offer clues to dark matter. Separately, scientists found rare, extremely salty, oxygen-free brine pools more than a mile beneath the Red Sea, hosting microbial life and offering possible clues to ancient oceans and extraterrestrial life.
