NASA’s Psyche spacecraft aces Mars flyby capturing stunning timelapse video ScienceDaily 科学

NASA's Psyche spacecraft successfully completed a Mars flyby, using the planet's gravity to speed toward a metal-rich asteroid (arrival 2029). During the flyby, it tested its cameras, magnetometer, and particle detectors, capturing unusual images and measuring Mars' magnetic environment, also spotting the planet's small moon.

Read more → Blazing like 10 billion suns: NASA’s Swift sees a wandering black hole devouring a star ScienceDaily 科学

NASA's Swift Observatory observed a supermassive black hole ripping apart a star more than 30,000 light-years from the center of a distant galaxy. The ultraviolet flare briefly outshone its host galaxy and revealed a black hole about a million times the Sun's mass.

Read more → Hidden charcoal reveals the true age of ancient cave paintings ScienceDaily 科学

A new analysis precisely dated paintings in France's Font-de-Gaume cave for the first time. Scientists discovered charcoal inside black pigments, enabling radiocarbon dating. Results show a bison was painted about 13,000 years ago, while sections of a mask-like figure were added during several winters.
