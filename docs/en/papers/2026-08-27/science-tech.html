NASA’s Roman Space Telescope is ready to hunt thousands of alien worlds ScienceDaily 科学

NASA’s Roman Space Telescope is now inside its Falcon Heavy fairing, meaning it is nearly ready for launch. The mission has the potential to uncover thousands of exoplanets and to help researchers investigate dark matter, dark energy and the accelerating expansion of the universe. That would mark a major stride in astrophysics.

Read more → One asteroid strike may explain two mysteries of Mars’ moon Deimos ScienceDaily 科学

New research suggests that an asteroid roughly 320 meters wide struck Mars’ moon Deimos at an angle, throwing debris around the entire world without breaking it apart. The event could explain both the moon’s giant southern depression and its unusually smooth, dusty surface. Observations from ESA’s Hera spacecraft support the scenario.

Read more → Scientists may have found a shortcut to calorie restriction’s anti-aging benefits ScienceDaily 科学

Moderate calorie restriction lowered levels of an immune protein called C3 that appears to contribute to chronic inflammation with age. Blocking the same protein reduced age-related inflammation in mice, suggesting that future treatments could mimic some benefits of calorie restriction without the need for severe dieting.

Read more → 190-year-old DNA reveals a hidden pangolin species ScienceDaily 科学

DNA from a nearly 190-year-old museum specimen has allowed scientists to confirm a distinct Himalayan pangolin species hiding in Nepal and northern India. The discovery could help trace illegally traded pangolin scales back to the species and the regions where they were poached. The work also demonstrates the value of museum collections for modern conservation.
