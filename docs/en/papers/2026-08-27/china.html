Four Departments Launch Special Campaign on Vehicle Reliability and Durability 中国新闻网时政

Four government departments, including the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology and the Ministry of Public Security, jointly issued a notice to launch a one-year special campaign focused on production consistency and quality improvement for road motor vehicles. Society & Law Briefing

In society and law, Shanghai released typical cases from its elevator safety governance campaign; Guangxi pioneered a three-inspector joint inspection for children's products; Jiangxi improved rural tap water coverage; and Wing Loong drones established an aerial lifeline in the Tibet disaster zone, showcasing technology-driven rescue capabilities.

Shanghai Releases Typical Cases in Elevator Safety Special Campaign 中国新闻网社会

Shanghai's market regulator has carried out a special governance campaign targeting the elevator industry, promoting smart elevator systems and releasing typical law-enforcement cases involving use management, maintenance, production and sales. The cases, made public on August 27, span the entire supply chain and aim to ensure public elevator safety.

Read more → Guangxi Pioneers "Three-Officer Joint Inspection" for Children's Product Safety 中国新闻网社会

Guangxi's market regulator announced a pioneering "three-inspector joint inspection" model combining product inspectors, samplers and regulators, to strengthen quality and safety supervision of children's products, improve enforcement efficiency and prevent substandard goods from reaching the market. It is the first such model in China and part of a campaign to safeguard children's consumer safety.

Read more → Jiangxi's Rural Tap Water Coverage Hits 95.5% in 2026 中国新闻网社会

Jiangxi Province reported that rural tap water coverage reached 95.5% in 2026, with 82.5% of rural residents served by scaled water supply projects and 91.6% under county-level unified management. More than 23,000 weak links have been resolved, marking a shift from basic access to quality water.

Read more → Wing Loong Drones Build "Aerial Lifeline" for Tibet Mudslide Rescue 中国新闻网社会

A Wing Loong drone, after completing its first mission in Shigatse, Tibet, took off again on August 27 to continue rescue operations in the Gyirong mudslide disaster. The two Wing Loong drones have established an "aerial lifeline" on the plateau, supporting disaster response.
