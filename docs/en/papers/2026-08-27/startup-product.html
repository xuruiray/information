New Products & Growth: A Wave of AI-Native Launches

Today's new-product stories focus on AI-native infrastructure and interfaces. Pluto turns a professional profile into an interactive AI agent that can join conversations and share opportunities. Speko describes itself as the OpenRouter for voice, offering developers a unified gateway to multiple voice models. Zhipu's GLM-5.3-Flash is the first natively multimodal model in the GLM-5 series, accepting text, images, and audio. HFlow provides scalable multimodal data pipelines for robotics, covering data collection, labeling, and orchestration for vision, language, and sensor inputs. These launches reflect a broader push toward agents, multimodal models, and physical-AI data readiness.

Pluto: Your Professional Profile Is Now an AI Agent Product Hunt

Pluto is a new product that transforms a user's professional profile into an interactive AI agent. Instead of a static resume or LinkedIn page, the agent can represent the user in discussions, respond to relevant opportunities, initiate connections, and help coordinate collaboration. The company positions this as a shift in personal branding: rather than merely presenting information, a profile becomes an active participant in professional conversations and networking. The launch is particularly interesting for people who want to scale their availability and engagement while keeping a human in the loop.

Read more → Speko: OpenRouter for Voice Product Hunt

Speko is launching as an OpenRouter-like gateway for voice models, giving developers a single API to access multiple speech and voice AI services. By abstracting away provider differences, it aims to lower the engineering cost of building voice-enabled applications and make it easier to switch models as new options appear. The product also provides routing, observability, and cost controls, similar to what OpenRouter does for text-based AI models. For startups building voice agents or voice user interfaces, Speko could become a core piece of infrastructure, reducing vendor lock-in and simplifying experimentation across speech recognition, generation, and understanding.

Funding & M&A: AI Hardware and Agent Startups Attract Capital

Capital continues to flow toward applied AI and open-web infrastructure. Legato emerged from stealth with $12 million and introduced AI-powered hearing glasses, integrating assistive audio into eyewear. Runable raised $21 million after seeing 60–70% of its token usage come from paying customers, betting that AI agents can move from building businesses to growing them. Flipboard is acquiring Graze, a startup focused on monetizing the open social web, to bring privacy-friendly advertising and creator tools into its platform. These moves suggest investors and acquirers are doubling down on hardware-enabled AI, agent-driven growth, and decentralized social monetization.

Hearing tech startup Legato emerges from stealth with $12M and a peek at its AI hearing glasses TechCrunch 创业

Legato, a hearing-technology startup, emerged from stealth with $12 million and a first look at its AI hearing glasses. The product, Legato Frames, embeds patented hearing-assistance tech into the arms of ordinary eyewear. By integrating microphones and directional audio, the company aims to reduce stigma and improve convenience for mild-to-moderate hearing loss. The glasses use adaptive algorithms to clarify speech in noise, and the new capital will support production, AI refinement, and market entry. The launch reflects a broader push to make assistive hearing more consumer-friendly and unobtrusive.

Read more → Runable hits $21M to bet AI agents can go from building businesses to growing them TechCrunch 创业

Runable has raised $21 million to push AI agents from business building into business growth. The startup says 60% to 70% of its trillion-plus tokens of usage in the past 90 days came from paying customers, indicating strong product-market fit. Its agents now handle customer acquisition, retention, and operational optimization, not just initial setup. The funding will expand agent capabilities, deepen integrations, and scale go-to-market. The high paid-usage ratio is a notable signal in a crowded AI-agent market.

Read more → Flipboard acquires Graze, the feed builder working to monetize the open social web TechCrunch 创业

Flipboard is acquiring Graze, a startup that helps users build custom feeds for Bluesky and other open social networks, focusing on monetizing the open social web. Graze developed privacy-friendly ad technology and creator monetization tools that Flipboard will integrate into its platform. The deal underscores Flipboard's ambition to become a central hub for distributed social content, giving independent creators and publishers more ways to earn revenue beyond centralized platforms. Combining Graze's feed-building product with its own editorial tools could strengthen the open social web's commercial ecosystem.
