Frontier Models: Medical, Legal, Chip Design, and Efficiency Advances

This subsection covers frontier LLM research, including a multi-turn diagnostic dataset for clinical evaluation, a mental health benchmark, a compact legal embedding model, adaptive mixed-precision quantization for efficient inference, and LLM-agent-designed algorithms for chip macro legalization. These works strengthen specialized evaluation and deployment efficiency.

MTDiag: A Multi-Turn Diagnostic Dataset Towards Clinically Meaningful LLM Evaluation arXiv 计算语言学

Clinical diagnosis is inherently interactive and incremental, yet current evaluation of medical LLMs relies on static QA or templated dialogues. MTDiag is a multi-turn diagnostic dataset designed to more realistically assess a model's ability to act as a dynamic clinical diagnostic agent, providing a clinically meaningful benchmark for medical AI.

Read more → HealthBench-Psych: A Mental Health Subset of OpenAI's HealthBench arXiv 计算语言学

Mental health is a pressing public-health concern as millions seek psychological support from LLMs. This work introduces HealthBench-Psych, a mental health subset of OpenAI's HealthBench, enabling isolated evaluation of model performance in this specialty and providing a granular tool for medical AI assessment.

Read more → GreenLeaf Law Embed Tiny: A Compact Embedding Model for Legal Domain Retrieval arXiv 机器学习

This paper introduces GreenLeaf Law Embed Tiny, a 0.6B-parameter embedding model for legal retrieval, achieving 75.11% on MLEB and 64.38% on MTEB(Law, v1), competitive among sub-1B models. A two-stage training approach enables efficient, lightweight legal search.

Read more → FAMPWQ: Fisher Information-based Adaptive Mixed Precision Weight Quantization for Effective LLM Inference arXiv 机器学习

Deploying large language models on resource-constrained devices is challenging; quantization is a key solution. FAMPWQ proposes Fisher information-based adaptive mixed-precision weight quantization, dynamically assigning precision based on parameter importance to reduce inference resources while maintaining performance.

Read more → MacroAgent: Regularity-Aware Macro Legalization with LLM-Agent-Designed Contour Algorithms arXiv 机器学习

Macro placement is central to VLSI design, and legalization greatly impacts quality. MacroAgent uses an LLM agent to design contour algorithms with explicit awareness of layout regularity, automating macro legalization and demonstrating LLM potential in complex engineering optimization.

Research & Governance: Safety and Robustness in Focus

This subsection features a comprehensive survey on multi-modal anomaly detection and a new method for balanced multimodal learning, addressing safety-critical applications and training stability for reliable AI deployment.

Multi-Modal Anomaly Detection: A Survey arXiv 机器学习

Multi-Modal Anomaly Detection (MMAD) identifies rare abnormal events from heterogeneous data sources, widely used in industrial inspection and cybersecurity. This survey systematically organizes fragmented literature across domains and modality combinations, analyzes key challenges, and provides a structured reference for future research.

Read more → CAT-GS: Balanced Multimodal Learning via Calibrated Gating and Fusion Surgery arXiv 机器学习

End-to-end training of multimodal networks often suffers from modality imbalance, unstable gating, and fusion degradation. CAT-GS introduces calibrated gating and fusion surgery to balance learning contributions across modalities, improving training stability and final performance.
