Development & Programming Briefing

This edition of Development & Programming focuses on AI’s growing influence on software development: an industry voice shares how AI generated and refined a million-line codebase into reliable software; the open-source community sees an early preview of the Qwen3.8-Flash-Next multimodal model, hinting at next-generation architecture; and GitHub releases a beginner’s Copilot tutorial that brings AI into everyday dependency management.
