Research & Governance Briefing

Research governance focuses on evaluation methodologies: consensus-based relative preference evaluation, user-conditioned personal agent assessment, cloud-native evaluation-as-a-service with conformal guarantees, and safety constraints in reinforcement learning.

A Consensus-Based Framework for Relative Preference Evaluation of Large Language Models arXiv 计算语言学

Static benchmarks fail to distinguish response quality; this paper proposes a consensus-based relative preference evaluation to capture clarity, completeness, and other dimensions.

Read more → Toward User-Conditioned Evaluation of Personal LLM Agents under Temporal Interventions arXiv 机器学习

Existing agent benchmarks evaluate tool use or memory in isolation; this paper proposes a user-conditioned framework that simulates agent evolution under temporal interventions.

Read more → Toward Goal-Agnostic Joint-Embedding Predictive Control of Partial Differential Equations arXiv 机器学习

Proposes a goal-agnostic control framework for PDEs using a joint-embedding predictive architecture, trained offline without reward and reusable.

Read more → Evaluation design conditions the expert-vs-auto MeSH gap: a controlled comparison of bag-of-words and BiomedBERT on the Cohen benchmark arXiv 计算语言学

In systematic reviews, MeSH assignments are either expert or automated. This paper compares bag-of-words and BiomedBERT on the Cohen benchmark, showing how evaluation design affects the gap.
