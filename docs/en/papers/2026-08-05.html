OpenClaw and Ollama in Agentic AI: Toward Fully Autonomous and Scalable AI Agent Systems

This paper systematically analyzes architectural gaps in the transition from reactive LLMs to persistent, action-capable systems, proposing separation of inference, orchestration, and execution layers, and integrating OpenClaw with Ollama to build fully autonomous, scalable agent systems. It offers a layered design reference for engineering autonomous agents.

DLLM-TTS: Block Discrete Diffusion Language Model for Text-to-Speech Synthesis Text-to-speech systems face a trade-off between intelligibility and efficiency. DLLM-TTS employs a block discrete diffusion language model to improve generation speed without sacrificing linguistic accuracy, presenting a promising alternative for high-quality synthesis. arXiv 计算语言学 How Hard Does It Think? Analyzing Step-Aware Reasoning Energy in LLM Chain-of-Thought Trajectories The study proposes a step-wise method to analyze computational effort in chain-of-thought trajectories, overcoming existing interpretability methods that rely on output-level signals or collapse processing depth. It helps identify key steps and redundant computation, offering new insight into how LLMs allocate reasoning energy. arXiv 人工智能 AgentMemBench: A Systematic Benchmark for Evaluating Long-Term Memory Management Strategies in Conversational AI Agents AgentMemBench evaluates five memory management strategies under identical conditions, addressing the critical bottleneck of coherent recall across thousands of conversational turns.
