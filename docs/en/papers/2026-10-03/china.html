China Watch Briefing

This edition of China Watch covers policy, industry and social developments during the National Day holiday. On policy, the traffic police authority reported orderly highways and issued safety reminders, the 169th China-Laos-Myanmar-Thailand joint Mekong patrol concluded, 13 pilot stations began allowing bicycles on high-speed trains, and Yangzhou promoted inbound travel under visa-free policies. Policy & Regulation Briefing

Policy news over the National Day holiday centered on transport and cross-border enforcement: the traffic police authority reported highway conditions and issued a travel safety advisory; the 169th China-Laos-Myanmar-Thailand Mekong joint patrol concluded; 13 high-speed rail stations were added to a bicycle-carrying pilot; and Yangzhou promoted inbound tourism under visa-free policies and convenience measures.

Traffic Remains Orderly on Second Day of National Day Holiday; Traffic Bureau Issues Safety Advisory 中国新闻网时政

China's traffic police said highway traffic volume fell on the second day of the National Day holiday, with major roads running in an orderly manner apart from slow stretches around large city clusters such as Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei, the Yangtze River Delta, the Pearl River Delta and Chengdu-Chongqing. Local authorities stepped up patrols, cracked down on speeding, overloading, fatigue driving and drunk driving, and issued safety reminders to holiday travelers.

Chinese vessel 53108 docked safely at the Jingha policing wharf in Xishuangbanna, Yunnan on Oct 2, concluding the 12-day 169th joint patrol and law-enforcement operation by China, Laos, Myanmar and Thailand on the Mekong River.

Read more → China Adds 13 High-Speed Rail Pilot Stations for Bicycle Carriage 中国新闻网社会

During the National Day holiday, China's railway operator added 13 pilot stations allowing passengers to bring bicycles aboard high-speed trains. The service is intended to make cross-city travel easier for cyclists, according to China News Service.

Read more → Visa-Free Policy and Convenience Services: Yangzhou Pitches 'China Travel' to Global Visitors 中国新闻网社会

The city of Yangzhou in Jiangsu is promoting itself to overseas travelers under the China Travel banner, citing visa-free policy benefits and improved convenience services, China News Service reported. The campaign aims to draw more inbound visitors to the city.

Industry & Local Briefing

Local industry and tourism news: Hangzhou opened a robot training school with its first 30 trainees; China's first 100-megawatt compressed CO2 energy storage project was connected to the grid in Mulei, Xinjiang; the Yungang Grottoes passed 5 million visitors for 2026 ahead of last year; and Dianyang Village in Fuzhou reopened with Fujian-Taiwan cooperation.

Hangzhou Opens Robot 'School' as First 30 Trainees Begin Hands-On Training 中国新闻网时政

Hangzhou has opened a robot-focused training school, where its first batch of 30 'students' are split by subject for hands-on drills and will take up posts after certification, China News Service reported. The program reflects demand for trained staff as robots are deployed across industries.

Read more → China's First 100-Megawatt Compressed CO2 Energy Storage Project Connects to Grid in Xinjiang 中国新闻网时政

China Huadian's compressed carbon dioxide energy storage project in Mulei, Xinjiang, was connected to the grid on Oct 2, becoming the country's first 100-megawatt-class project of its kind and opening a green, zero-carbon route for large-capacity storage, China News Service reported.

Read more → Yungang Grottoes Pass 5 Million Visitors Earlier Than Last Year 中国新闻网时政

The Yungang Grottoes scenic area in Datong, Shanxi, received more than 5 million visitors in 2026 by 11:19 am on Oct 2, 23 days earlier than last year, according to the Yungang Research Institute.

Read more → Fuzhou's Dianyang Village Welcomes Visitors as Fujian and Taiwan Build Rural Renewal 中国新闻网社会

Dianyang Village in Fuzhou, Fujian, has reopened to visitors after renovation, with Fujian-Taiwan cooperation shaping a new rural landscape, China News Service reported. The project is presented as a model for rural tourism and revitalization.

Demand for At-Home Pet Feeding Services Surges, Raising Risk Concerns 中国新闻网社会

During the National Day holiday, many people traveling or returning home turned to at-home pet feeding services instead of traditional boarding. Rising demand has drawn attention to the risks of such new services, including safety and liability issues, China News Service reported.

Daqing drilling crews worked through the night at oilfields during the National Day holiday to keep production going, according to China News Service. Their round-the-clock shifts were framed as a mark of responsibility among frontline energy workers.

Cultural tourism remained popular over the National Day holiday, with Zhengzhou's 'urban archaeology experience class' drawing visitors. At the site, the public tried archaeological tools, watched artifact restoration and joined interactive activities, China News Service reported.

The 18th Sihong International Dayuantang fishing contest opened in Sihong, Jiangsu, with nearly 1,000 anglers from across China taking part. Cong Zhihui of Zhoukou, Henan, won the one-million-yuan top prize, followed by Ren Junfang of Heyuan, Guangdong, and Dai Qixian of Neijiang, Sichuan.
