NHC: 1,000 More Primary Health Institutions to Offer Pediatric Care This Year 中国新闻网时政

At a State Council Information Office briefing, National Health Commission head Lei Hai Chao said all tier-2 and tier-3 public general hospitals offered pediatric services last year, and 90% of community health centers and township clinics provided pediatric care. This year China aims to add 1,000 more primary institutions offering such services, targeting over 95% coverage by year-end. About 3,100 pediatricians have been dispatched from higher-level hospitals to support grassroots facilities.

Read more → China Releases Four Mandatory National Standards for Mine Safety 中国新闻网时政

China's market regulator and national standards committee approved four mandatory national standards for mine safety, including rules for surveying hidden disaster-causing factors and technical requirements for lithium-ion power batteries used in underground mines. Organized by the National Mine Safety Administration, the standards take effect on March 1, 2027, providing a unified mandatory technical benchmark for risk control and equipment safety.

A Film and a Digital Library: Reading Charity Reaches Over 10,000 Schools 中国新闻网社会

The 'Excellent Films on Campus' and 'Scholarly China' public welfare event was held on Sept 13 at a primary school in Huailai County, Zhangjiakou, Hebei. Through charitable donations, the program provides films and digital library resources and has reached more than 10,000 schools.

The first catenary wire on the Chongqing section of the Chengdu-Chongqing Middle Line high-speed railway was successfully installed in Dazu District, Chongqing, the Yangtze River Coastal Railway Group's Chongqing company said on Sept 13. Built by China Railway Electrification Engineering Group, the installation marks a key phase in the line's electrification and signaling works.
