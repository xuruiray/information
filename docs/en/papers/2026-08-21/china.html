Read more → NBS: China's Early Rice Output Remains Stable in 2026 中国新闻网时政

China's National Bureau of Statistics said on Aug. 21 that early rice output remained stable this year despite heavy rains and typhoons. Based on sample surveys in 10 provinces, the planted area edged up while yield per unit fell slightly. Total output reached 56.58 billion jin, staying above 56 billion jin for the sixth consecutive year.

Setting Safety Boundaries for AI Companions 中国新闻网社会

The Interim Measures for the Administration of Anthropomorphic AI Interactive Services have taken effect, setting safety boundaries for AI-powered companionship. A commentary on Chinanews.com says the regulation helps govern human-machine emotional interaction and clarifies responsibilities for service providers.

Read more → Chengdu Officials Warn Over 'Big Fish' Claim Downstream of Xinglong Lake 中国新闻网社会

After the Xinglong Lake reservoir in Chengdu discharged floodwater, claims of 'big fish freely available' downstream gained attention online. Local officials issued a reminder, urging the public to stay rational and prioritize personal safety, and cautioning against taking unnecessary risks.

Read more → Early Screening for Autism Calls for Whole-Society Support 中国新闻网社会

A Chinanews.com article calls for joint efforts to promote early screening and intervention for autism. It urges removing cognitive barriers and strengthening institutional support. The article says families, medical institutions and society should work together to support children with autism.

Read more → Egg Pancake Shop Owner Declines Donations, Wants 'Love Relay' to Continue 中国新闻网社会

In Hefei, Anhui province, egg pancake vendor Wang Xianhuang found a 100-yuan note placed in his takeout box while counting orders on the evening of Aug. 20. When netizens offered to transfer money to support him, the shopkeeper declined, saying he hoped the kindness would be passed on.
