AI & LLMs Briefing

This edition of the AI & LLMs briefing focuses on agentic systems and model infrastructure. New work spans synthetic game-development agents, radiotherapy workflow orchestration, key-layer KV communication for multi-agent collaboration, and elastic prefill-decode disaggregated LLM serving. In research and governance, papers examine evidence-governed discovery, bounded autonomy and verifiable safety for agentic automation, sycophancy under user pressure, and detection of emotionally rewritten fake news. Together, the selections show a field moving from single-model demos toward governed, production-ready agent workflows.

Frontier Models

The Frontier Models subsection highlights agent training and model-system efficiency. It covers synthetic trajectories for game-development agents, an LLM-orchestrated radiotherapy care pathway, key-layer KV communication for multi-agent collaboration, governed natural-language dashboard generation over enterprise data, and elastic scheduling for disaggregated prefill-decode LLM serving. These works emphasize orchestration, communication, and serving efficiency.

GAMEGO: Training Game-Dev Agents with Synthetic Trajectories Anchored in Real-World Assets arXiv 人工智能

The paper introduces GAMEGO, training game-development agents with synthetic trajectories anchored in real-world assets. It targets browser-based game generation, a prominent frontier for LLM front-end execution, and explores training and evaluation beyond complex multi-turn workflows or static game benchmarks.

Read more → RadOnc-Agent: An LLM-Orchestrated Framework for AI Workflows Across the Radiotherapy Care Pathway arXiv 人工智能

The paper presents RadOnc-Agent, an LLM-orchestrated framework for AI workflows across the radiotherapy care pathway. It connects fragmented AI capabilities spanning clinical stages, software environments, and data modalities, from treatment decision-making through follow-up, aiming to support longitudinal radiotherapy workflows.

Read more → Task-Oriented Key-Layer KV Communication for Efficient Latent Multi-Agent Collaboration arXiv 机器学习

The paper studies LLM-based multi-agent systems and proposes task-oriented key-layer KV communication. It transmits latent internal states instead of natural-language messages to reduce inference costs, addressing limitations in existing KV-based latent communication methods that prioritize sender-side state fidelity.

Read more → Text2Dashboard: A Governed Agent Architecture for Natural-Language Dashboard Generation over Enterprise DataBrain arXiv 人工智能

The paper introduces Text2Dashboard, a DataBrain-specific prototype that turns natural-language analytic requests into inspectable dashboards. An installable Codex plugin and standalone Agent Runtime combine schema-constrained model decisions with typed tools, persistent state, and deterministic hooks for approval, audit, and checks.

Read more → FluidPD: In-Place Elasticity for SLO-Aware Prefill-Decode Disaggregated LLM Serving arXiv 人工智能

The paper addresses prefill-decode disaggregated LLM serving with FluidPD. Existing systems often combine a fixed prefill/decode worker ratio with request routing, while real-world workloads fluctuate; this work explores in-place elasticity for SLO-aware serving.

Research & Governance

Research & Governance

The Research & Governance subsection examines verifiable and auditable AI systems. It covers evidence-governed search for AI research agents, bounded autonomy and verifiable safety for agentic automation, how task, model, and pressure shape sycophancy, and detection of emotionally rewritten fake news. Together, these papers address reliability, safety, evaluation, and deployment concerns for AI behavior.

EPOCH: Reliable Discovery through Evidence-Governed Search arXiv 人工智能

The paper examines AI research agents that search over programs, mathematical constructions, and proofs. Existing systems often optimize evaluator feedback without governing how it is interpreted, challenged, and reused, allowing fragile candidates to persist; EPOCH proposes evidence-governed search.

Read more → Bounded Autonomy and Verifiable Safety for Agentic AI Enabled Automation arXiv 机器学习

The paper argues that agentic AI automation cannot be safely deployed in high-stakes environments on probabilistic reasoning alone. It discusses epistemic drift, where deeper reasoning may move behavior away from expert safety constraints, and presents BRaVeS, a bounded reasoning framework for verifiable safety.

Read more → Beyond the Sycophancy Score: How Task, Model, and Pressure Shape LLM Yielding arXiv 计算语言学

The paper studies sycophancy in large language models, where models may abandon a correct answer or endorse a user's position after pushback. Arguing that a single rate per model says little about when this happens, the authors analyze how task, model, and pressure shape LLM yielding.

Read more → Leveraging LLM-Generated Explanations for Detecting Emotionally Rewritten Fake News arXiv 计算语言学

The paper addresses the social impact of fake news. Existing detection methods often rely on stylistic variation or external information, but news articles are frequently rewritten under different emotional backgrounds while preserving underlying facts; the work explores LLM-generated explanations for detecting such emotionally rewritten fake news.
