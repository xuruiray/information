Physicists just found the 'ghost' of superconductivity ScienceDaily 科学

Physicists have found evidence of an unusual form of superconducting behavior in uranium ditelluride, where electron pairs organize into rippling patterns known as pair density waves. Notably, these patterns appear to persist even after ordinary superconductivity fades, offering new clues about unconventional superconductivity.

Read more → Webb finds a surprising atmosphere on a scorching lava world ScienceDaily 科学

The James Webb Space Telescope has found strong evidence that the rocky lava world HD 3167 b retains an atmosphere despite orbiting its star every 24 hours under extreme heat. The discovery could help scientists understand how rocky planets keep their atmospheres and what a young, molten Earth may have looked like.

Earth's center of mass shifts by a few millimeters as vast amounts of water, ice and even air migrate with the seasons. NASA scientists have developed a more precise way to track these tiny swings using laser-ranging satellites, GPS data and measurements of how water and ice deform Earth's crust.
