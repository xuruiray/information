China Watch Briefing

This edition of China Watch focuses on discipline and policy developments. Society & Law

During the National Day holiday, overall public security remained stable, with 15.462 million border crossings, up 6.7% year-on-year, and Guangzhou reporting a 13.6% fall in criminal cases. China also became home to the world's first ISO technical committee for artificial ice and snow landscapes, while the Datong-Qinhuangdao Railway began autumn maintenance to secure winter energy transport.

World's First ISO Technical Committee on Artificial Ice and Snow Landscapes Established in China 中国新闻网社会

Harbin Ice and Snow World Co., Ltd. has been approved to host the secretariat and domestic technical counterpart of the ISO technical committee on artificial ice and snow landscapes (ISO/TC358), the first such international standardization body in the field.

Read more → Major Energy Corridor Datong-Qinhuangdao Railway Begins Autumn Maintenance 中国新闻网社会

Starting October 7, the Datong-Qinhuangdao Railway, known as China's No. 1 heavy-haul line, began a 20-day autumn intensive maintenance program to restore track and equipment after peak summer operations and prepare for winter coal transport.

Read more → China Records 15.462 Million Border Crossings During National Day Holiday, Up 6.7% 中国新闻网社会

Border inspection agencies handled 15.462 million inbound and outbound trips during the National Day holiday, averaging 2.209 million per day, up 6.7% year-on-year. The single-day peak reached 2.397 million on October 4.

Read more → Guangzhou Reports 13.6% Year-on-Year Drop in Criminal Cases During National Day Holiday 中国新闻网社会

Guangzhou police reported that criminal case reports fell 13.6% year-on-year during the 2026 National Day holiday, while traffic-related reports dropped 3.38%. Major events and key scenic areas remained orderly, with overall public security stable.
