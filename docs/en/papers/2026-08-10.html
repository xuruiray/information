Lead Story
OpenAI says it slowed Astra model development over security concerns

OpenAI said its in-development Astra model had reached a "critical cybersecurity threshold," meaning it could independently identify and carry out cyberattacks against traditionally well-protected real-world systems. The company has slowed development to address safety concerns and stated it will not deploy the model until it is deemed safe. This underscores the growing tension between frontier AI capability and security safeguards.
