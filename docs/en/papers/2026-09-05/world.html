UN votes to adopt new world map to reflect Africa's true size BBC 国际

The U.N. voted to adopt a resolution phasing out the traditional Mercator map, which shrinks Africa’s apparent size, in favour of a projection that reflects reality. Togo sponsored the resolution; 164 countries backed it, while the United States cast the only no vote. The shift highlights contested views about how the world is seen.

The body of Oyo, widely described as the world’s youngest traditional king, returned to Uganda following his death in the United States at 34 from aggressive cancer. His funeral is expected to draw crowds, but behind it lies a dispute over succession that could test the authority of Uganda’s traditional institutions.

Rescue teams in Nepal have praised a 'rare moment of joy' after pulling survivors from tunnels along the Trishuli River, but hundreds of workers remain trapped after severe flooding. The delicate rescue operation is continuing under difficult conditions, with each new extraction raising hopes that more lives can be saved.
