Landslide in Suichuan County, Jiangxi Leaves 1 Dead, 11 Missing 中国新闻网时政

A mudslide triggered by days of heavy rain from Typhoon Saddle hit Shixia Group, Mingkeng Village, Gaoping Town, Suichuan County, early Sept. 5, damaging 12 houses, eight of them completely. Three people were rescued — two in good condition and one dead — while 11 remain missing as rescue operations continue.
