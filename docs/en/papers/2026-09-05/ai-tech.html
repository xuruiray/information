Google’s Gemini Spark can now manage your Google Photos library TechCrunch AI

Google has expanded Gemini Spark to Google Photos, letting subscribers on AI Pro or Ultra edit and curate photo albums, create shared collections, and turn images into calendar events. The update positions Gemini as a practical everyday assistant for personal media, widening the surface area of agentic features inside Google’s consumer ecosystem.

Read more → The sameness problem behind those unappetizing AI-generated menus TechCrunch AI

This TechCrunch analysis examines why AI-generated restaurant menus can leave customers feeling uneasy. While generative tools promise cheap copy and imagery, the output often carries a sameness that consumers sense viscerally. The article warns that convenience-driven AI menus may erode brand distinctiveness and appetite rather than improve the dining experience.

Read more → Abliteration.ai is making a business out of removing AI guardrails TechCrunch AI

Abliteration.AI is building a business around easier access to powerful AI models stripped of guardrails. The company argues that equipping defenders with the same capabilities as attackers can strengthen cybersecurity overall. Critics worry about opening a marketplace for unsafe models, highlighting unresolved tensions between open access, safety, and abuse prevention in frontier AI.
