New Products & Growth: Slow Messaging, Speed Reading, and Terminal AI

This edition's new products span three directions: Hey Postcard brings slow messaging through randomized next-morning delivery, Glyphi improves reading efficiency with RSVP, and Muse Code gives developers a terminal agent for long-horizon coding. Together they touch communication, learning, and programming, making this a useful section for product enthusiasts. All three launched within the past day and are available for early users to explore.

Hey Postcard: Digital Postcards Delivered at a Random Time the Next Morning Product Hunt

Hey Postcard is a digital postcard app built around the idea of slow messaging. When you send a card, it is not delivered immediately. Instead, it arrives the next morning at a random time between 8 AM and 10 AM, adding a sense of anticipation and ritual to digital communication. The product is listed on Product Hunt for early users to explore.

Read more → Glyphi: A Speed Reader for Books, PDFs, and Web Pages Product Hunt

Glyphi is an RSVP speed-reading tool that works with books, PDFs, articles, and web pages. It displays words one at a time in a fixed position, guiding the reader's eye across the screen to increase speed while preserving comprehension. For people who consume large volumes of text, the tool promises a more efficient reading experience, and it is available now on Product Hunt.

Read more → Muse Code: Meta's Terminal Agent for Long-Horizon Coding Product Hunt

Muse Code is a terminal agent from Meta built for long-horizon, multi-step coding tasks. It can autonomously execute code actions inside the terminal, reducing the repetitive manual work developers face on large projects and helping them deliver more quickly. The release signals Meta's continued push into AI-assisted developer tooling, and the product is now listed on Product Hunt.
