23 Young Taiwanese Legal Professionals Complete Internship Program in Shanghai 中国新闻网时政

The 2026 Taiwan Youth Legal Talent Practice Base concluded its one-month program at East China University of Political Science and Law, with 23 young Taiwanese professionals successfully completing their internships. The initiative offers Taiwan's legal talent a platform to understand the mainland's legal environment and practical operations, fostering cross-strait youth exchange and professional integration.

Read more → China-Led International Standard for Heat Treatment of Ferrous Metals Released 中国新闻网时政

The International Organization for Standardization has published a China-led revision of the fundamental heat-treatment standard "Ferrous metals — Heat treatment — Vocabulary." Chinese experts served as working group convener and project leader, with German, Japanese, Finnish, French and Italian experts participating. It is China's first international standard led in this foundational field, boosting its global industrial voice.

The forums focused on integrating AI with aviation technology and building consensus among academia and industry.

Society & Law: Flood Alerts, Public Service and Cultural Heritage

This subsection covers Beijing's waterlogging risk alert, the Xinzhou blood station's response to public criticism, the green ammonia project in Jilin, and tech-driven cultural heritage preservation in the Three Gorges. The stories span urban disaster prevention, public service accountability, green development, and cultural heritage protection, reflecting the fabric of social governance and local life.

Green Ammonia Powered by Wind and Solar: Jilin Town Exports Worldwide 中国新闻网社会

Located in the Da'an clean-energy chemical industrial park in western Jilin, the SPIC "Hydrogen Oasis · Da'an Green Ammonia" demonstration project uses local wind and solar resources to produce green ammonia, exporting to multiple countries. The project combines clean-energy conversion with chemical industry upgrading, making it a new calling card for green development in northeast China.

Read more → Xinzhou Blood Station Responds to Allegations of Blocking Shelter from Rain 中国新闻网社会

After netizens reported that a blood donation station in Xinzhou, Shanxi blocked people from taking shelter during heavy rain, the city's central blood station responded via its official WeChat account, explaining the situation and apologizing. It said it would improve services and open facilities during storms. The incident highlights the importance of emergency responsiveness in public service institutions.

Read more → Beijing Issues Blue Alert for Waterlogging Risk 中国新闻网社会

Beijing's water authority and weather bureau jointly issued a blue waterlogging risk alert. From noon Aug 7 to 8 a.m. Aug 8, Haidian and Shijingshan face moderate risk of waterlogging, while other urban areas face general risk. The bureau reminded residents to monitor weather changes, avoid low-lying areas, and protect underground spaces.

Read more → Three Gorges Heritage Revitalized Through Technology 中国新闻网社会

During the "Ten-Year Yangtze Journey" report, journalists found that the Three Gorges region is preserving cultural heritage through digital scanning, AI restoration and other technologies, bringing historical sites to life. These technological tools enable more efficient documentation and display of precious artifacts, offering a new path for sustainable cultural heritage preservation.
