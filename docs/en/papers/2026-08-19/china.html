Policy & Regulation

This section covers key policy moves and achievements: three ministries issued a notice to channel college graduates to community posts; assisted reproduction under medical insurance aided over 1.7 million people in the first half; "Most Beautiful Doctors" stories were released for Chinese Doctors' Day; and the Zhuque-3 rocket achieved China's first land recovery, marking a space breakthrough.

Three Ministries Jointly Issue Notice to Boost Graduate Employment in Urban and Rural Communities 中国新闻网时政

The Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security, the Central Social Work Department, and the Ministry of Civil Affairs jointly issued a notice on optimizing urban and rural community posts to attract more college graduates, providing talent support for modernizing grassroots governance. The policy is part of broader efforts to stabilize employment and strengthen community services.

Read more → Medical Insurance Assisted Reproduction Benefits Over 1.7 Million Person-Times in First Half 中国新闻网时政

Since January 2025, all 31 provincial-level regions and the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps have included assisted reproduction in medical insurance reimbursement. Over the past year and a half, the policy has benefited more than 1.7 million person-times in the first half of this year, reducing the financial burden on families.

Read more → Publicity Department and National Health Commission Release 'Most Beautiful Doctors' Stories 中国新闻网时政

On the occasion of Chinese Doctors' Day, the Publicity Department of the CPC Central Committee and the National Health Commission publicly released the advanced stories of the "Most Beautiful Doctors," aiming to foster respect for doctors and enhance efforts to build a Healthy China.

Read more → China Achieves First Land Recovery of a Rocket 中国新闻网时政

On August 19, the Zhuque-3 Y2 rocket lifted off from the Dongfeng Commercial Aerospace Innovation Zone, with its first stage landing at the Minqin recovery site. It became the first Chinese rocket to reach orbit and achieve land recovery. The success marks a major advance in reusable rocket technology.

Society & Law

This section covers a fatal wall collapse at a graduation banquet in Sichuan, two earthquakes in Qinghai, a court hearing on an alleged university admission fraud, and healthcare data showing 5.29 million licensed doctors. These stories highlight public safety, disaster response, legal integrity and health progress.

Five Dead After Wall Collapse at School Banquet in Sichuan's Changning County 中国新闻网社会

On August 18, a wall supporting a temporary canopy collapsed at a family graduation banquet in Changning County, Sichuan, after rainwater accumulated excessively. Five people died and 17 were injured, according to local authorities. The accident has prompted calls for improved safety at outdoor events.

Read more → Two Earthquakes Strike Da Qaidam, Qinghai, Felt Strongly Locally 中国新闻网社会

At 5:36 a.m. on August 19, a 5.6-magnitude earthquake struck Da Qaidam in Haixi Prefecture, Qinghai, at a depth of 10 km, followed by multiple aftershocks. Strong shaking was felt locally, and the provincial earthquake agency has deployed personnel; no casualties have been reported.

Read more → Court Hears Case of Alleged University Admission Fraud by Brother 中国新闻网社会

On August 18, Tang Shida, the brother accused of allegedly taking another person's university admission, faced an administrative lawsuit against Xiangtan University in court, with his brother Tang Yida appearing as a third party. The case centers on the legality of degree revocation and has drawn widespread attention.

Read more → China's Licensed Doctors Reach 5.29 Million, Health Indicators at Best Level 中国新闻网社会

By the end of 2025, China had 5.29 million licensed (assistant) physicians, exceeding the target set in the 14th Five-Year Plan. In 2025, medical institutions recorded over 10.6 billion visits, and residents' key health indicators reached the best level in history.
