Scientists may have finally proved that “empty” space isn’t really empty ScienceDaily 科学

Using a magnetar's colossal magnetic field, scientists may have caught a quantum effect predicted by Werner Heisenberg nearly 90 years ago, in which seemingly empty space alters light's behavior. If confirmed, it would provide the first direct evidence of vacuum birefringence and open a new window into quantum-vacuum physics.
