Inference-Time Mitigation of Adversarial Political Bias in Large Language Models arXiv 计算语言学

Researchers propose a new method to mitigate adversarial political bias in large language models at inference time, aiming to keep outputs non-partisan in information retrieval and summarization. The study argues that current alignment paradigms remain insufficient and that inference-time intervention offers a complementary safeguard, highlighting such protections as essential for safer, trustworthy AI.

The study presents a multi-modal generative fuzzy system that uses fuzzy inference to guide large-model interactive question answering. It jointly encodes text, images, and speech, aiming to improve complex semantic reasoning and decision-making across heterogeneous information. This architecture offers a novel alternative to conventional deep learning approaches for multimodal QA.

Read more → The Unwritten Benchmark: A New Challenge for Multimodal Machine Learning in Abstract Perceptual Reasoning arXiv 人工智能

The Unwritten Benchmark challenges multimodal models to infer unseen information from dynamic generative processes, probing abstract perceptual reasoning beyond static recognition. Research & Governance

This subsection highlights research at the intersection of AI safety and governance: HarmProfile characterizes harmful output distributions in frontier LLMs; a position paper argues moral evaluations should cover both values and norms; new work addresses safety alignment in low-resource languages, uncertainty signals for systematic-review screening, and communication gating in multi-agent RL.

HarmProfile: Characterizing Harmful Distributions in Frontier LLMs arXiv 计算语言学

HarmProfile treats harmful generation in frontier LLMs as an object of analysis rather than merely an attack outcome. The framework uses large-scale, high-quality collections to characterize harmful output distributions, offering a systematic tool for safety evaluation and aiding understanding of model misbehavior.

Read more → Position: Evaluations of AI Moral Reasoning Still Miss Half of the Picture arXiv 人工智能

This position paper argues that moral reasoning evaluations overemphasize whether model outputs align with human values, while neglecting the moral norm problem—whether models identify and apply context-sensitive norms. It calls for a more complete evaluation framework that captures both halves of moral competence.

Read more → Auxiliary uncertainty signals for LLM-assisted systematic review screening: a benchmark across eight Cohen drug-class reviews arXiv 计算语言学

Across eight Cohen drug-class reviews, the study shows that an auxiliary BERT+GCN classifier supplies structured uncertainty signals that improve LLM-assisted title-abstract screening efficiency. It also identifies how prompt-delivery methods affect screening outcomes, advancing reliable AI-assisted evidence synthesis.

Read more → LLM Safety Alignment in Low-Resource Languages: A Systematic Literature Review arXiv 计算语言学

This systematic literature review examines LLM safety alignment in low-resource and multilingual settings, finding that safety guarantees are significantly weaker than in high-resource languages. It highlights the urgent need for stronger safety evaluation and alignment research across low-resource languages, with important implications for global AI governance.

Read more → When to Communicate: Belief Distributions and KL Divergence for Principled Gating in Multi-Agent RL arXiv 人工智能

The paper introduces a principled communication gate based on belief distributions and KL divergence, enabling agents in multi-agent RL to decide when to communicate. It avoids the instability of per-step communication and high-variance REINFORCE gating, improving cooperative performance and offering a more robust approach to communication decision-making.
