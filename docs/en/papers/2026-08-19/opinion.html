Community Discussion Briefing

The Community Discussion section features three high-engagement essays: 'The Amazon tax' on platform power, 'Norway should buy OpenAI' on AI sovereignty, and 'Beware Management Consultants' questioning consulting value. Each has sparked extensive debate among readers.

The Amazon tax Hacker News

Seth Godin introduces the concept of 'The Amazon tax,' examining the role of e-commerce giants in transactions and their potential impact on merchants, consumers, and market competition. The article drew nearly a thousand upvotes and hundreds of comments, sparking vigorous debate.

Read more → Norway should buy OpenAI Hacker News

The article proposes that Norway, as a sovereign state, should acquire OpenAI, arguing that state ownership could align AI development with public interests. It ignited broad discussion on AI governance and technological sovereignty, with over 200 comments reflecting polarized views.

Read more → Beware Management Consultants Hacker News

A commentary from UK supermarket Iceland's website warns companies to be cautious about management consultants, questioning their high fees and real value based on the firm's own experience. The post sparked heated debate on corporate decision-making and external consulting, earning many upvotes and comments.
