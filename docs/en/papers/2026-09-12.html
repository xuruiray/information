Lead Story
Don't sleep on wrapture

Graham Dumpleton’s new monkey-patching package wrapture is shaping up as an indispensable tool for Python developers. Simon Willison notes that despite near-daily tutorials since its August 31 release, the project has received little buzz. It offers safer, more convenient ways to wrap and replace functions and classes, making it relevant for teams focused on runtime extensibility and maintainability.
