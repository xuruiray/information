Youth Journalists from Central Media Launch 'Four Abilities' Training in Harbin 中国新闻网社会

A mobilization meeting for the 'Four Abilities' education practice for young journalists from central media was held in Harbin on July 30. Organized by the China Journalists Association and the Publicity Department of Heilongjiang Provincial Party Committee, the program gathered more than 30 young journalist representatives from central and local media outlets to enhance their professional capabilities.

Read more → Chengdu High-tech Zone Reports River Foam Incident, Water Quality Back to Standard 中国新闻网社会

The Ecological Environment and Urban Management Bureau of Chengdu High-tech Zone reported that white foam appeared at the junction of the Gaopan River and Jinjiang River, as netizens had noted. The authority responded immediately, removing the foam the same day. Professional testing later confirmed that water quality has returned to standard levels.

Read more → Vietnamese Blogger Explores Yunnan: Ancient Alleys and Calm Villages 中国新闻网社会

At Banqiao Town in Baoshan, Yunnan Province, Vietnamese social media blogger Xiaoqing filmed local delicacies and streets, saying she was recording life and experiencing a different way of life. Her exploration highlights the cultural charm of Yunnan's border areas and promotes people-to-people exchanges between China and Vietnam.

Read more → 2026 'Xinjiang is a Good Place' Intangible Heritage Event Opens 中国新闻网社会

The 2026 'Xinjiang is a Good Place' intangible cultural heritage aid-Xinjiang exhibition opened in Shihezi on July 30. The event brings together 651 representative intangible heritage projects and 549 inheritors from 31 provincial-level regions and the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps, making it the largest in the event's history.
