What if the brain does not create consciousness? ScienceDaily 科学

Neuroscientist Christof Koch tackles the mystery of whether the brain actually creates consciousness. He argues that materialism has failed to solve this 'hard problem' and plans to investigate puzzling phenomena like near-death experiences and terminal lucidity. His work points toward the possibility that consciousness may not originate directly from neural activity, opening a controversial but intriguing pathway for future research.

Read more → This 4,000-year-old city defied the rules of history ScienceDaily 科学

Analysis of the ancient city Mohenjo-daro reveals that as it prospered, the gap between its richest and poorest households actually narrowed. Its communal infrastructure, equitable trade systems, and absence of powerful royal elites suggest that social equality may have been a key ingredient in the city’s remarkable success.

Read more → Scientists found evolution repeating itself in the Galápagos Islands ScienceDaily 科学

In the Galápagos Islands, giant daisies repeatedly evolved similar heat-tolerant leaf shapes, yet each lineage used a distinct set of genes to achieve it. Researchers also uncovered substantial genetic differences between isolated populations, hinting that new species may be in the making—a vivid example of convergent evolution and ongoing speciation.

Read more → A forgotten fossil just revealed a new Triassic predator from 210 million years ago ScienceDaily 科学

A fossil overlooked for nearly 80 years in New Mexico has been identified as a previously unknown crocodile relative, Eosphorosuchus lacrimosa, living 210 million years ago. The jackal-sized predator boasted a reinforced skull, powerful jaw muscles, and a short snout designed to tackle large prey, reshaping our picture of Triassic ecosystems.
