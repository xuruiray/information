Community Discussion Briefing

Community Discussion covers three highly engaged topics: a Google app ad experiment where most installs appeared to be bots, concerns about misalignment of AI in mathematics, and the open-sourcing of Rune. These threads reflect developer interest in ad platform credibility, the reliability of AI in scientific research, and the evolving open-source tooling ecosystem.

I spent $220 on Google app ads and 60% of the installs were robots Hacker News

The author documents a Google app ad experiment, reporting that after spending $220, roughly 60% of installs came from bots. The post sparked community discussion about traffic quality, fraud detection, and the reliability of install metrics on major ad platforms, with many commenters sharing similar experiences.
