Society & Law Briefing

The Society & Law subsection focuses on online rumor governance, controlled drug management, AI campus health applications and Yellow River ecological operation. Jiayuguan police reported a fabricated funding threat case; new rules on discharge medications for narcotic and psychotropic drugs will take effect; a Bozhou school canteen introduced AI constitution analysis; and joint sediment flushing began at key Yellow River reservoirs.

'Funding Cut Leads to Threat' Story Reversed; Yao Suspected of Picking Quarrels and Provoking Trouble 中国新闻网社会

Jiayuguan police reported that a 37-year-old man surnamed Yao fabricated a story about being threatened after stopping funding a student in order to attract online attention. He is now suspected of picking quarrels and provoking trouble. Police said the case shows efforts to curb fabricated online content and maintain public order.

Read more → New Rules on Discharge Medications for Narcotic and Psychotropic Drugs to Take Effect 中国新闻网社会

Narcotic and psychotropic drugs are essential for pain relief and sedation but can cause dependence and addiction and pose severe risks if diverted. New rules on discharge medications aim to meet clinical needs while strengthening whole-process management. The policy is expected to affect hospitals, patients and drug supervision.

Read more → AI Tongue Diagnosis for Body Constitution Brings 'Health Mode' to School Canteens in Bozhou, Anhui 中国新闻网社会

A middle school in Bozhou, Anhui, has installed AI health terminals in its canteen. Students stick out their tongues and receive a body constitution report in about two seconds, which the canteen uses to offer more personalized meal suggestions. The system is part of a campus health pilot.

Read more → 2026 Joint Sediment Flushing Operation Starts at Key Reservoirs on Upper and Middle Yellow River 中国新闻网社会

The 2026 joint sediment flushing operation began on the upper and middle Yellow River. Liujiaxia Hydropower Plant will increase outflow to 1,320 cubic meters per second for seven days, coordinating with Longyangxia and Liujiaxia reservoirs to flush sediment at six reservoirs including Shapotou and Qingtongxia.
