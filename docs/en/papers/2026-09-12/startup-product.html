LiveGrid Launches to Watch Multiple Live Streams at Once Product Hunt

LiveGrid, a new product on Product Hunt, allows users to watch several live streams at the same time in a single view, so they do not miss real-time content across multiple channels. The tool is aimed at multi-stream monitoring and content consumption scenarios, and it was featured among the platform's latest launches.

India's Pocket FM has doubled its revenue run rate to $500 million, according to TechCrunch. The company uses AI to produce 99% of its new content, which helps make content production roughly 80 times cheaper, showing how AI can reshape audio content economics.
