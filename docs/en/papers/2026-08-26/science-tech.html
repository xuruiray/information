Atomic catalyst unlocks the hidden value of plant waste ScienceDaily 科学

Scientists have created a highly efficient catalyst that breaks down stubborn lignin from plant waste into useful chemicals under relatively mild conditions. By revealing exactly how the catalyst works at the atomic level, the discovery could help turn forestry and agricultural waste into renewable building blocks for fuels, plastics, and other materials.

Read more → NASA finds Earth microbes could survive on the Moon ScienceDaily 科学

NASA scientists found that microbes brought by astronauts could survive in cold, shadowed niches around the Moon’s South Pole, potentially complicating future searches for pristine lunar chemistry. Some refuges may be no larger than a footprint, while one hardy fungus could even withstand limited exposure to sunlight.

Read more → Three forgotten kings could rewrite the history of ancient Assyria ScienceDaily 科学

A new analysis of ancient tablets, inscriptions and a statue suggests that three forgotten Assyrian kings may have been hiding in plain sight for more than 2,700 years. Researchers argue that these rulers were omitted from the famous Assyrian King List, a finding that could reshape the political history of ancient Assyria.

Read more → Your brain may not actually “make” decisions ScienceDaily 科学

A neuroscientist challenges the long-held idea that the brain contains a central system that actually makes our decisions. Instead, what we experience as choosing may emerge from the continuous interaction of perception, movement, the body, and the surrounding world, a view that could change how we think about free will and responsibility.
