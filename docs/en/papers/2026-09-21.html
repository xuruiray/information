China, US Hold Trade Talks in New York, With Dialogue on AI

On September 20, Chinese Vice Premier He Lifeng met US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Trade Representative Jamieson Greer in New York for China-US trade talks. The two sides discussed implementing previous consensus and boosting bilateral trade and investment, and held a dedicated dialogue on artificial intelligence. The talks were described as candid, in-depth and constructive.
