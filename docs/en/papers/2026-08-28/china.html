Society & Law Briefing

This subsection highlights Typhoon Sader defense, organized crime sentencing, national patent awards, and commercial judicial practice. Fujian evacuated over 240,000; Tianjin handed down verdicts in four gang cases; the 26th China Patent Awards honored 697 projects; Guangdong released top commercial and financial cases.

26th China Patent Awards Released, 697 Projects Honored 中国新闻网社会

China's National Intellectual Property Administration announced the winners of the 26th China Patent Awards. A total of 697 projects received gold, silver and excellence awards for invention patents and design patents, showcasing the country's innovation achievements.

Read more → Tianjin Cracks Down on Gang Crimes: 4 Cases Sentenced, 20 Defendants Convicted 中国新闻网社会

Courts in Tianjin pronounced verdicts on four gang-related cases, involving criminal groups and cliques. Twenty defendants received first-instance sentences ranging from 17 years to one year and one month in prison, highlighting the city's continued fight against organized crime.

Read more → Fujian Mobilizes Against Typhoon Sader, Evacuates Over 240,000 中国新闻网社会

Fujian Province launched full-scale defense against Typhoon Sader, the 18th typhoon of the year, evacuating 247,700 people as of 7 a.m. on Aug. 28. Authorities strengthened maritime and land safety controls amid risks from double typhoons, consecutive rainfall and astronomical tides.

Read more → Guangdong Courts Mediated Nearly 2.53 Million Commercial and Financial Cases in Three Years 中国新闻网社会

Guangdong High People's Court released the top ten commercial and financial cases of 2025, covering capital markets, corporate governance, financing and new business insurance. The court said Guangdong courts mediated nearly 2.53 million commercial and financial cases over the past three years.
