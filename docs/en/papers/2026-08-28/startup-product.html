New Products & Growth: Multi-Model Prompt Testing and 'Second Brain' Agent

Two AI products caught our attention on Product Hunt today. Revalvo is a developer utility that allows running the same prompt across multiple models simultaneously, then scores, versions, and ships the best outputs—a practical answer to model comparison fatigue. Almanac takes a different approach, positioning itself as an AI agent with a 'second brain,' where long-term memory and knowledge retrieval enable more coherent multi-step tasks. Both reflect an ongoing shift in AI product design: moving away from simple chat interfaces and toward structured workflow and memory-rich agents.

Almanac Product Hunt

Almanac is introduced as an AI agent with a 'second brain'—a persistent knowledge layer that stores conversations, documents, and learnings for later retrieval. Instead of treating each session as a fresh start, Almanac gives the agent long-term memory and context, allowing it to handle complex, multi-step tasks more consistently. The product represents a growing category of AI agents that combine reasoning with structured personal and team knowledge, positioning memory as the key differentiator.

Funding & M&A: Three Early-Stage Rounds in Consumer, Pet, and Infrastructure

The funding news this edition covers three early-stage rounds that illustrate different ways startups are attacking incumbent industries. Atorie’s $9.5 million raise uses direct factory sourcing to deliver luxury-quality goods without retail markups. Hoomanely combines a smart feeding bowl with an AI health platform to monitor pets’ eating behavior and alert owners to early signs of illness. And a startup led by a former PG&E engineer has secured $26 million in Series A funding to build a 'Google Maps for the underground,' aiming to reduce utility-construction delays and improve safety. Together, these deals show investor appetite for models that remove cost, add intelligence, or streamline infrastructure.

Fashion startup Atorie raises $9.5M to bring consumers luxury goods without the markup TechCrunch 创业

Fashion startup Atorie has raised $9.5 million to bring luxury goods to consumers without the usual retail markup. The company’s website lets shoppers purchase handbags and clothing made from the same materials—and coming from the same factories—that manufacturers use for high-end brands. By sourcing directly from the supply chain and cutting out traditional distribution layers, Atorie aims to offer the quality and craftsmanship of luxury products at significantly lower prices. The funding will help expand its product categories and strengthen the factory-direct platform.

Read more → Hoomanely’s building a smart feeding bowl and an AI platform to help owners spot when their pup is sick TechCrunch 创业

Hoomanely is developing a smart feeding bowl and an AI-powered platform to help pet owners monitor their dog’s health. The bowl measures and records feeding data, such as how much and how fast the dog eats, while the AI tracks behavioral changes over time. When the system detects deviations from normal patterns, it alerts the owner early, potentially catching illness before visible symptoms appear. The company is betting that combining hardware with machine learning creates a compelling value proposition for the growing pet-tech market.

Read more → This former PG&E engineer is building a ‘Google Maps for the underground’ TechCrunch 创业

A startup founded by a former PG&E engineer is building what it calls 'Google Maps for the underground'—a digital map of buried utilities and infrastructure. The company recently raised a $26 million Series A to expand its customer base and help reduce red tape for utility and construction work. By providing accurate, up-to-date ground-level data, the platform aims to help contractors avoid accidental strikes, speed up permitting, and improve safety on dig sites. The funding will be used to add more geographic coverage and develop additional software features.
