Frontier Models: Capability Gains Paired with Risk

Frontier work this edition ties capability gains to risk. A review of LLM-enabled, agentic GeoAI maps ethical and privacy governance gaps; a biosecurity paper assesses model capabilities and threat pathways across research workflows. Three method papers add symbolic policy learning for generalizable visual generation, metacognitive steering for scientific judgment, and self-reflective visual coding for chart replication.

Toward Governance-Aware Autonomous GIS: A Narrative Review of Ethical and Privacy Risks in LLM-Enabled GeoAI arXiv 人工智能

The paper reviews how LLM-powered GeoAI expands the querying, generation, and interpretation of spatial information through natural-language interfaces and agentic workflows, and argues these capabilities create governance challenges that general AI ethics debates do not fully address, calling for governance-aware autonomous GIS.

Read more → Artificial intelligence and biosecurity: capabilities, threat pathways, and defense-in-depth governance arXiv 人工智能

The paper maps AI capabilities across the digital-to-physical biosecurity workflow, noting general-purpose LLMs can retrieve and integrate scientific information and support experimental planning and computational analysis, while biological foundation models add predictive power, and proposes defense-in-depth governance.

Read more → OmniHarness: Harnessing Generalizable Visual Generation via Symbolic Policy Learning arXiv 机器学习

The paper identifies three limits in unified multimodal LLMs and multi-agent visual generation: limited generalization from task-specific experience, reflection deferred until task completion, and hard-to-reuse knowledge. It proposes symbolic policy learning to make visual generation more generalizable.

Read more → Metacognitive Steering: Learning the Structure of Scientific Judgment arXiv 人工智能

The paper argues long-horizon scientific discovery requires agents to alternate between exploration, disciplined execution, and critical reassessment as evidence changes, yet current models train mostly on the products of science with outcome-level signals, offering limited supervision, and proposes metacognitive steering.

Read more → ViCo: Visual-oriented Coding with Self-Reflection for Chart Replication arXiv 计算语言学

The paper targets academic charts that match human visual standards, noting that while existing AI agents can produce well-structured text and code, their visualizations lag in stylistic and semantic fidelity, and proposes visual-oriented coding with self-reflection.

Research & Governance

Research & Governance: From Strategic Wargames to Base Methods

This thread spans governance and foundational methods. A position paper argues language models are not ready for open-ended strategic wargames; a bias-audit study finds ten instruments disagree on ranking frontier models. Research items cover training-free activity recognition, action-precondition placement in neuro-symbolic RL, and position-constrained span masking for pre-training.

You Don't Need To Train: Agentic Heuristic Learning Studio for Executable Human Activity Recognition arXiv 机器学习

The paper argues human activity recognition need not rely on gradient-based neural training. Its Agentic Heuristic Learning Studio mirrors how people learn activities by remembering examples, forming rules, and repairing mistakes, exploring a training-free path to executable recognition.

Read more → Position: AI Is Not Ready for Strategic Conflicts arXiv 人工智能

This position paper frames open-ended strategic wargames as high-stakes LM-based social simulations that model adversaries, institutions, escalation, plan brittleness, and crisis response, arguing current language models are not ready to play agents or adjudicate ambiguous actions.

Read more → Bias Audits Detect Bias but Disagree on Ranking: Evidence from Ten Instruments and Ten Frontier Models arXiv 计算语言学

The paper notes that emerging AI regulation mandates bias audits of high-risk systems and that audit scores are increasingly used to rank models, both assuming different tools measure the same thing. It runs ten extrinsic audit instruments over ten frontier models to test that assumption directly.

Read more → Managing Action Preconditions in Neuro-Symbolic RL: Three Placement Strategies for Embodied Agents arXiv 机器学习

Analogizing to how humans carry behavior knowledge from familiar situations into new tasks, the paper argues RL agents need not relearn known behavior patterns, and compares three placement strategies for action preconditions in neuro-symbolic reinforcement learning.

Read more → POSPAN: Position-Constrained Span Masking for Language Model Pre-training arXiv 机器学习

Reviewing the advantages of span-level masked language modeling in pre-training, the paper notes that prior work considers only span length under discrete distributions while ignoring dependencies, and proposes position-constrained span masking to improve pre-training.
