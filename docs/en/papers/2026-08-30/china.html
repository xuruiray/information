China Coast Guard Patrols Territorial Waters and Surrounding Areas of Huangyan Island 中国新闻网时政

The China Coast Guard said on August 30 it conducted law-enforcement patrols in the territorial waters of Huangyan Island and surrounding areas. Since August, it has strengthened control and handled illegal vessels in accordance with the law to safeguard sovereignty and maritime rights. The move underscores China's continued assertion of territorial sovereignty.

Read more → 261 Foreign Nationals from 23 Countries Unaccounted for After Jilong Landslide in Tibet 中国新闻网时政

Tibet's regional government said at a press conference on August 30 that 261 foreign nationals from 23 countries remain unaccounted for after the Jilong landslide. Search and rescue efforts are continuing. Authorities are carrying out verification and coordinating with foreign missions. The landslide occurred in Gyirong County, prompting an emergency response.

Read more → Landslide Below Purepuqiang Tsangpo Barrier Lake Could Form New Lake 中国新闻网社会

The Ministry of Emergency Management said drones are monitoring the Purepuqiang Tsangpo barrier lake after a landslide downstream began forming a new barrier lake. Authorities are assessing risks to support rescue efforts for the Jilong disaster in Tibet. The situation adds complexity to the ongoing disaster response near the China-Nepal border.
