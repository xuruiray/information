FAST Detects Largest Atomic Gas Structure Ever Seen in Universe 新华网科技

Using the Five-hundred-meter Aperture Spherical radio Telescope (FAST), astronomers observed the compact galaxy group Stephan’s Quintet and its surroundings, uncovering a giant atomic hydrogen structure spanning about 2 million light-years—roughly 20 times the size of the Milky Way. It is the largest atomic gas structure ever detected, offering new clues about galaxy evolution.

On Oct 7 at 21:10, the Taiyuan Satellite Launch Center used a Long March-11 sea-launched rocket from the Yellow Sea to send two SpaceSense BDS low-orbit navigation augmentation test satellites (S5/S6) into orbit. The mission succeeded, with satellites entering their planned orbits, furthering tests for enhanced Beidou navigation services.

Read more → Wentian Lab Module Docks with Tianhe Core Module 新华网科技

The Wentian lab module successfully docked with the Tianhe core module combination, marking another key milestone in China’s space station construction. The Shenzhou-14 crew will enter Wentian for subsequent in-orbit operations, adding major capacity for scientific experiments on the station.

Read more → China Successfully Launches ChinaSat 9B Satellite 新华网科技

At 19:50 on Sept 9, China launched the ChinaSat 9B satellite aboard a Long March-3B rocket from the Xichang Satellite Launch Center. The satellite entered its planned orbit successfully. ChinaSat 9B is expected to provide direct broadcast and television services across China, expanding the country’s communications satellite fleet.

NASA’s Roman Telescope Will See 100 Times More Sky Than Hubble ScienceDaily 科学

NASA’s Roman Space Telescope will combine Hubble-like detail with a field of view 100 times larger, giving astronomers a sweeping new way to explore the cosmos. Its observations could uncover hidden planets, map billions of cosmic objects, and help scientists unravel the mysteries of dark matter and dark energy.
