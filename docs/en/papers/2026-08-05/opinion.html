Keyv and Related npm Packages Hit by Active 'Shai-Hulud' Supply-Chain Attack Hacker News

Security firm Aikido reports that Keyv and related npm packages are under an active supply-chain attack codenamed Shai-Hulud, with malicious versions published that may affect downstream projects. The disclosure urges maintainers and developers to upgrade immediately, audit lockfiles, and check for signs of credential compromise.
