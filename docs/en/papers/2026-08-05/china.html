China Watch Briefing

China sees major advances in policy and technology today. Six ministries issued a document to strengthen management of court-employed clerical staff, a Jielong-3 rocket successfully launched two hyperspectral satellites, Fujian marked the eighth anniversary of water supply to Kinmen with a cumulative volume exceeding 50.7 million tonnes, and the High-Intensity Heavy-Ion Accelerator Facility produced and identified nuclide hafnium-153 for the first time. In society, Generation Z is diving into the silver economy, Western youths are increasingly drawn to Chinese culture, the Puppet Manchukuo Palace Museum has become a popular tourism site, and scientists confirmed a carnivorous plant lineage on the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau. These stories reflect China's ongoing progress in rule of law, innovation, people's livelihood, and diverse social trends.
