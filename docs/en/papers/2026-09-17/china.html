At its briefing on the 15th Five-Year Plan, the National Radio and Television Administration signaled tighter rules: AI-generated broadcast and online programs must be labeled, key production steps must keep human review, and AI “deep edits” are prohibited. It reported 430,000 micro-dramas in the first eight months, over 90 percent AI-made, and said drama production should reduce reliance on traffic stars. Jiangxi's Dayu County Says 37 Pigs That Died in Transit Did Not Enter the Market 中国新闻网社会

After online posts alleged dead pigs at a Dayu Dongjin Food facility in Jiangxi, the county agriculture bureau and public security departments opened an overnight investigation. A notice issued on September 17 said the 37 pigs that died during transport did not enter the market.

Read more → Sichuan's Maoxian County Evacuates 255 Before Landslide Hits 中国新闻网社会

At about 8:40 a.m. on September 16, a landslide of roughly 800,000 cubic meters hit Shidaguan village in Diexi town, Maoxian county, Sichuan, blocking part of National Highway 213. Early patrols, monitoring and warnings allowed authorities to evacuate residents and control traffic in advance, averting potential casualties among 255 people.

Read more → Guizhou Driver Detained After Hitting Pedestrians, Injuring Five in Qingzhen 中国新闻网社会

Qingzhen police in Guizhou said a 44-year-old driver, surnamed Wang, hit pedestrians on a crosswalk on Yunling West Road at 12:29 p.m. on September 16, injuring five. All were hospitalized; one required surgery and is in stable condition. The driver has been detained for investigation.
