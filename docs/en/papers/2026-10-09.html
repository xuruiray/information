Lead Story
China's Manus raises over $500M in first funding round since split with Meta

TechCrunch reports that Manus, a Chinese AI company, has raised more than $500 million in its first funding round since separating from Meta. Boyu Capital and IDG Capital led the round, while existing shareholders Tencent, HSG — formerly known as Sequoia China — and ZhenFund, among others, also participated. The round marks a significant vote of confidence in the company after the split and highlights continued investor interest in Chinese AI ventures.
