9th International Estuarine and Coastal Conference Opens in Qinzhou, Focusing on AI and Coastal Evolution 中国新闻网社会

The 9th International Estuarine and Coastal Conference opened on September 23 in Qinzhou, Guangxi, under the theme "From Tradition to AI: Estuarine and Coastal Evolution and Modern Civilization." About 300 experts and scholars from 22 countries across five continents are attending to discuss coastal science and AI applications.

Read more → Foreign Students in Chongqing Experience Mid-Autumn Culture by Making Mooncakes and Exploring Intangible Heritage 中国新闻网社会

Ahead of the Mid-Autumn Festival, foreign students including Yemeni student Wan Sheng'ao from Chongqing University of Technology took part in a cultural experience event in Chongqing, making mooncakes and exploring intangible heritage. Wan said mooncakes are not only tasty but symbolize reunion in Chinese culture, and it was his first Mid-Autumn Festival in China.

Read more → Zhuang and Yao Communities in Lianshan, Guangdong Celebrate Harvest with Songs and Rice Fields 中国新闻网社会

On September 23, Lianshan Zhuang and Yao Autonomous County in Qingyuan, Guangdong, held a 2026 Chinese Farmers' Harvest Festival event at the Shiping Zhuang-Yao Cultural Tribe in Yonghe Town. Visitors and locals gathered for songs, dances, markets, farming contests and food, showcasing the harvest in an ethnic region.

Read more → "Choose Dad or Mom?" Judges Listen to Children's True Wishes in Divorce Cases 中国新闻网社会

In divorce cases involving minors, custody decisions often require hearing the child's views. Under Chinese law, for children over two, courts decide based on the child's best interests if parents cannot agree; for those over eight, their true wishes should be respected. The report shows how judges listen to minors.
