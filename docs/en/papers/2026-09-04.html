Lead Story
GPT-6 Astra: OpenAI's next-generation model starts rolling out to limited organizations

OpenAI has unveiled GPT-6 Astra, rolling out today to a limited set of organizations and, over coming days, to all ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users as well as through the OpenAI API and AWS. The model is priced at the same rate as Claude on the API. Simon Willison notes he has not tried it yet, framing the launch as the beginning of a broader availability rollout.

OpenAI launches Astra, its powerful (and controversial) new model OpenAI has launched Astra, its new AI model, calling it a new frontier in computer and browser use. The company says Astra combines speed, accuracy, and safety on agentic tasks, but the launch is also generating debate over how much control such models should have over user interfaces and online environments. TechCrunch AI Meta is paying to peek at how you use their latest AI model Meta is offering an explicit discount of roughly 95% on average for Muse Spark, its new model for coding and agent applications, to users who contribute prompts and model outputs to future-model development. The strategy effectively pays users to share real-world usage data, prompting debate over data pricing and model-training transparency. TechCrunch AI Legora reviewed 41 documents in minutes with GPT-6 Astra Financial software company Legora used GPT-6 Astra to review 41 financial documents in minutes, locating all four planted errors. According to OpenAI, performance in the financial-review workflow improved by nearly 40%.
