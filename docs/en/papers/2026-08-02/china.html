Society & Law: Flood Alerts, Consumer Scams, and Safety Accountability

The Songhua River saw its first flood of 2026, prompting water authority warnings. The China Consumers Association exposed fake 'miracle cures' peddled in private livestreams. Songhua River Sees First Flood of 2026 中国新闻网社会

Due to rainfall, the water level at Jiamusi Hydrological Station on the Songhua River rose to 79.49 meters, and the flood was designated as 'Songhua River Flood No. 1 of 2026'. The Ministry of Water Resources issued the information and reminded downstream areas to take precautions.

Read more → Fake 'Miracle Doctors' and 'Magic Drugs' Hide in Private Livestreams: Beware of Phony Experts, Inflated Popularity, and Real Scams 中国新闻网社会

The China Consumers Association has received recent complaints about pharmaceutical ads in private channels that falsely use medical personnel to promote 'miracle cures', which are often just health foods or ordinary foods. Consumers realize they were scammed only after seeking offline medical advice, highlighting the need for vigilance.
