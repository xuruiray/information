Space, Energy & Robotics Briefing

This subsection covers quantum technology and space. Harvard researchers continuously surrounded a diamond-based qubit with microscopic sound waves, extending coherence time roughly threefold, while another team cut thousands of repeated control cycles down to one, speeding certain quantum operations more than a thousandfold. In space, new simulations suggest an intact Moon could form within hours of a giant impact, and NASA-derived search-and-rescue technology has saved five lives.

Tiny sound waves could help solve a major quantum computing problem ScienceDaily 技术

Researchers at Harvard demonstrated a way to protect quantum information using microscopic sound waves. By continuously surrounding a diamond-based qubit with mechanical vibrations, they extended its coherence time by roughly threefold. The same phonons could eventually both transmit and protect quantum information, opening the door to compact sound-based quantum architectures.
