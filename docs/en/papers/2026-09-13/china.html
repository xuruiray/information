Society & Law Briefing

This subsection covers social welfare and rule of law. As heavy rain hit Hainan from a tropical depression, the Ministry of Water Resources activated a Level IV flood response; a report highlighted public security at the 'first school in Tibet' in Chamdo; the world's first pterosaur museum opened in Hami, Xinjiang; and police launched a review after questions over responsibility in a fatal drunk-driving case involving a public official in Pu County, Shanxi.

By the Lancang River: The 'First School in Tibet' and Its Public Security Story 中国新闻网社会

Chamdo's Chama Square by the Lancang River is a popular local spot where residents and tourists dance Guozhuang and shops welcome visitors. The report recounts the public security practices of the 'first school in Tibet.'

Read more → Ministry of Water Resources Activates Level IV Flood Defense Response for Hainan 中国新闻网社会

China's Ministry of Water Resources activated a Level IV flood defense response for Hainan as heavy rain is forecast from Sept 13 to 15 due to a South China Sea tropical depression. The Nandu, Changhua and Wanquan rivers are expected to rise notably, and some small and medium rivers may exceed warning levels.

Read more → World's First Pterosaur Museum Opens in Hami, Xinjiang 中国新闻网社会

The world's first pterosaur museum, the Hami Pterosaur Museum, opened in Hami, Xinjiang on Sept 13, according to the Institute of Vertebrate Paleontology and Paleoanthropology of the Chinese Academy of Sciences. The Hami Pterosaur-Yardang National Geopark was also unveiled.

Read more → Public Official's Drunk Driving Kills Couple; Responsibility Finding Questioned, Police Launch Review 中国新闻网社会

Police in Pu County, Shanxi, issued a notice on the night of Sept 12 regarding an Aug 13 traffic accident in which a township public official allegedly drove drunk and killed a couple. The victims' family questioned the finding on primary and secondary responsibility and applied for a review.
