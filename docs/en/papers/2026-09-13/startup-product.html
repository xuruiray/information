India’s Pocket FM doubles revenue run rate to $500M as AI powers 93% of audio content TechCrunch 创业

Pocket FM said its revenue run rate has doubled to $500 million. The India-based audio platform uses AI to produce 99% of its new content, making content production about 80 times cheaper. According to the company, AI powers 93% of its audio content. The figures highlight how generative AI is reshaping audio entertainment economics and content volume at scale.

The three 28-year-old founders behind energy startup Furo moved from Silicon Valley back to Germany. Despite the move, the company secured $4 million in funding from mostly U.S. backers. The case suggests that founders of energy technology startups may not need to stay in Silicon Valley to attract capital and build their companies.
