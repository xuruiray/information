The authors argue that mechanistic interpretability is central both to the science of deep learning and to trustworthy deployment, and that existing approaches to interpreting representations remain limited.

Security Team Demos WeWorm, a Zero-Click Worm Spreading via WeChat Calls Simon Willison

A research team released a demo of WeWorm, which it describes as the first zero-click worm to spread through WeChat calls on iOS and Android. Victims do not need to answer the call or interact with their phone at all, and the exploit still succeeds even if they answer and hear nothing. The team said it used AI to find the bug and write the first remote exploit code.
