Society & Law Briefing

Society and law coverage focuses on enforcement and grassroots practice. Twenty-five provincial-level regions simultaneously destroyed counterfeit and substandard goods, and Kunming police detained a man for fabricating a food-safety rumour. Meanwhile, Walu Village in Yinchuan and Jiulun Village in Hunan's Xiangxiang illustrate rural revitalization through shared-prosperity mechanisms and a growing chili-sauce industry.

Nationwide Unified Destruction of Counterfeit and Substandard Goods Held 中国新闻网社会

On 10 September, the office coordinating the national quality-power strategy and the State Administration for Market Regulation jointly organized the 2026 nationwide destruction of counterfeit and substandard goods, carried out simultaneously in 25 provincial-level regions, with SAMR Vice Minister Bai Qingyuan attending.

Police in Kunming's Yangzonghai scenic area said on 10 September that an online claim about someone spitting in flower cakes was fabricated, and that the 30-year-old man surnamed Du who posted it had been placed under administrative detention.
