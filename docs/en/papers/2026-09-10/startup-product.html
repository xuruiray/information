Harvey hits $15.5B valuation, months after reaching $11B TechCrunch 创业

The legal AI startup Harvey has reached a $15.5 billion valuation, nearly doubling its valuation from $11 billion within nine months. As a venture capital favorite, the company has drawn significant investor interest, reflecting continued momentum in legal AI tools.

Read more → Uber invests $10M in Indian fleet operator Carrum at $168M valuation TechCrunch 创业

Uber has invested $10 million in Indian fleet operator Carrum Mobility at a $168 million valuation. Carrum operates about 5,100 vehicles in India and is Uber's largest fleet partner for its premium Uber Black service in the country. The deal underscores Uber's focus on fleet partnerships in India.

Sequoia is doubling down on Cymphony, which gives security teams a single view of employees, AI agents, and other nonhuman identities, including the systems and sensitive data they can access. The investment comes as AI agents create new enterprise security risks.
