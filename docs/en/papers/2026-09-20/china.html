White Bamboo Shoots 'Bleached Overnight'? Closing Loopholes in Agricultural Processing Oversight 中国新闻网社会

A recent short-video exposé revealed illegal workshops in mountainous areas of Qingyuan, Guangdong, using sulfur fumigation to bleach bamboo shoots overnight and extend their shelf life. The case has raised concerns about regulatory gaps in agricultural product processing.

At about 11 p.m. on September 18, the Yangjiang coast guard received a report that a fishing boat had run aground on rocks near Dongping Xifanshi, endangering 11 fishermen. A patrol boat was dispatched and rescued all aboard despite rough nighttime conditions.
