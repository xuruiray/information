Geopolitics & Security Briefing

The Middle East front continues to widen: Iran has set terms for talks including ending the war and lifting the US naval blockade, Saudi Arabia says it foiled an attack on Riyadh, and Houthi rebels tried to strike the Saudi capital with ballistic missiles. The destruction of cultural heritage in conflicts also draws attention.

Cultural treasures are being destroyed by war, and people want justice BBC 国际

Across multiple conflicts, cultural monuments and heritage sites are being destroyed. Activists, researchers and local communities are documenting the losses and pressing for accountability, arguing that protecting cultural heritage is a shared human responsibility. They warn that the loss of heritage erases memory and identity, and that deliberate destruction can amount to a war crime. International courts and UNESCO are being urged to act.

Global Society Briefing

In Minna, Nigeria, 37 suspected illegal goldminers — many of them children — died in a prison cell while in the custody of a paramilitary group. Survivors recount panic and a struggle to breathe, and police fired teargas as protests broke out. The deaths raise questions about detention conditions, children's rights and law enforcement accountability.
