Frontier Models Briefing

The frontier models subsection focuses on new model releases and agent applications. Mistral introduced Mistral Large 4, a 1T-parameter multimodal model. Research papers cover synthetic trajectories for game-development agents, radiotherapy pathway orchestration, zero-shot corpus visualization and scalable driver-identification embeddings.

Mistral’s new 1T model aims to leapfrog closed and open rivals TechCrunch AI

French AI lab Mistral AI released Mistral Large 4, a new large multimodal model that aims to leapfrog both American and Chinese rivals. The 1T-parameter model enters a crowded race among closed and open model developers, drawing industry attention. It is positioned as a major European challenger in the global frontier model market.

Read more → GAMEGO: Training Game-Dev Agents with Synthetic Trajectories Anchored in Real-World Assets arXiv 人工智能

The paper introduces GAMEGO, which trains game-development agents with synthetic trajectories anchored in real-world assets. It targets browser-based game generation, a prominent frontier for large language models, and explores ways to reduce reliance on complex multi-turn workflows.

Read more → RadOnc-Agent: An LLM-Orchestrated Framework for AI Workflows Across the Radiotherapy Care Pathway arXiv 人工智能

The paper presents RadOnc-Agent, an LLM-orchestrated framework for AI workflows across the radiotherapy care pathway. It seeks to integrate capabilities fragmented across clinical stages, software environments and data modalities, covering the longitudinal process from treatment decision-making to follow-up. The system aims to connect otherwise isolated AI tools in clinical practice.

Read more → Zero-Shot Visualization: Exploring Text Corpora with User-Prompted Axes arXiv 计算语言学

The study introduces zero-shot visualization, a task in which users specify concepts in natural language and documents are mapped onto corresponding concept axes. The approach aims to help researchers visually explore large textual corpora without task-specific training. It examines how large language models can support interactive corpus analysis.

Read more → TEMPEST: Temporal Embeddings for Scalable Driver Identification via Angular Margin Learning arXiv 机器学习

The paper introduces TEMPEST, a temporal convolution and angular margin learning approach for scalable driver identification. It aims to maintain discriminative performance as fleet size grows and reduce overfitting to session-specific patterns under rigorous temporal evaluation. Existing triplet-loss formulations often degrade rapidly in such settings.

Research & Governance

5 articles
Research & Governance Briefing

The research and governance subsection focuses on evaluation, alignment and reproducibility. Papers introduce distributional aggregation for judge models, a bias-variance theory for guidance-augmented reasoning, actionable latent structures, evidence-governed search, and a 4-billion-parameter open model for calibrated randomized-trial answers with a license-clean release.

JudgeMoE: Distributional Aggregation for LLM-as-a-Judge arXiv 计算语言学

The paper introduces JudgeMoE, a lightweight aggregator that assigns example-specific weights to cached judge score distributions and fuses them before computing a final score. It aims to retain uncertainty and disagreement information that is lost after scalar compression. The method is designed for large-language-model-as-a-judge settings.

Read more → When Does External Guidance Help LLM Reasoning? A Bias-Variance Theory of Guidance-Augmented GRPO arXiv 机器学习

The paper examines when external guidance helps large-language-model reasoning in reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards. It proposes a bias-variance theory of guidance-augmented GRPO, analyzing whether expert traces, self-explanations or retrieved thought paths improve multi-step reasoning or introduce bias. The work aims to clarify the conditions for effective guidance.

Read more → Capacity, Responsiveness and Alignment: What Makes a Latent Structure Actionable arXiv 计算语言学

The study examines the localization and control of latent structures in language-model activation space. It asks what makes a localized structure actionable, analyzing why some structures have stronger causal influence and proposing dimensions such as capacity, responsiveness and alignment. The work contributes to understanding and steering model behavior.

Read more → EPOCH: Reliable Discovery through Evidence-Governed Search arXiv 人工智能

The paper presents EPOCH, an evidence-governed search approach for AI research agents. It focuses on how evaluator feedback is interpreted, challenged and reused, aiming to prevent fragile candidates from being prematurely promoted in searches over programs, mathematical constructions and proofs. It addresses reliability in automated discovery.

Read more → Calibrated Answers About Randomized Trials From a 4-Billion-Parameter Open Model: A Registered Test and a License-Clean Release arXiv 计算语言学

The paper presents Fiorillo v0.5, an open model that answers typed questions about randomized trials with a probability for each answer. It reports a registered test and a license-clean release, emphasizing calibration and reproducibility in medical question answering. The specialist model reads trial articles and classifies intervention effects.
