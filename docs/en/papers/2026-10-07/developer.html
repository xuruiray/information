Infrastructure & Data Briefing

This subsection focuses on Meta's NTS authenticated time service, now live at nts.meta.com. Packets are authenticated so devices can verify origin and integrity, servers hold no per-client state, cookie keys are derived rather than stored, and the implementation is fully open sourced.

Meta Launches NTS Authenticated Time Service and Open Sources the Implementation Meta 工程

Meta's public time service now supports NTS (Network Time Security, RFC 8915) at nts.meta.com. Packets are authenticated so devices can verify the time came from Meta and was not modified in transit. Servers hold no per-client state, cookie keys are derived rather than stored or replicated, and the implementation is open sourced.
