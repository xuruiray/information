China's Supreme People's Court Releases 2026 Typical Antitrust Cases

China's Supreme People's Court on September 10 released six typical antitrust cases aimed at clarifying market competition rules and promoting a fair-competition legal environment. The release coincided with China's 2026 Fair Competition Policy Promotion Week. The cases are meant to guide courts and regulators in applying antitrust law and addressing market conduct. They underscore the judiciary's role in supporting fair competition as part of the country's broader push to strengthen competition policy enforcement.
