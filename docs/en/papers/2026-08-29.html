Lead Story
Just a rumour of a bug is enough to find a security exploit these days

Anil Madhavapeddy, professor of computer science at Cambridge and core maintainer of OCaml, reports that security issues in OCaml projects are seeing attempted exploits within minutes of patches being shared for discussion. This alarming trend highlights that even mere rumors of a bug are enough to trigger attacks, underscoring the need for faster security responses and the growing threats faced by open-source maintainers.
