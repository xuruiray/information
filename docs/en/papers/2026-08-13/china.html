Society & Law: Swift Law Enforcement and Flood Response

Taiyuan police detained a man for covert filming in a commercial area, and a court sentenced 17 people for fraud. A tourist abuse case in Ulanqab was resolved with an apology, while Henan activated the Nihewa flood basin to reduce peak flows, reflecting orderly social governance and emergency response.

Man Detained for Taking Unauthorized Photos of Women in Commercial Area, Taiyuan Police Say 中国新闻网社会

According to a police bulletin issued on August 12 by the Yingze Branch of the Taiyuan Public Security Bureau, officers investigated and detained a man who was reported to have taken covert photos of women in the Liuxiang commercial area. The police statement said that after learning about the situation from netizens, the bureau quickly organized forces to handle the case, underlining the authorities' firm stance against privacy violations in public places.

Read more → 17 Sentenced for Fraud in Fake 'National Project' Promising 3.8 Million Yuan Returns 中国新闻网社会

A court has sentenced 17 people for fraud after they allegedly ran a fake 'national project' scheme. The gang fabricated official documents and promised huge returns, claiming that supporting the so-called project would yield a dividend of 3.8 million yuan. The verdicts serve as a warning against schemes that exploit patriotism, reminding the public to be wary of unofficial 'national project' investment offers.

Read more → Tourist Abused After Declining Horse Ride: Staff Apologize in Writing, Service Suspended 中国新闻网社会

A video showing a tourist being verbally abused and threatened by a horse farm worker near Huanghuagou in Ulanqab, Inner Mongolia, drew public attention. After the incident, local culture and tourism authorities, together with the police and township officials, quickly stepped in. The worker later gave a written apology and was forgiven by the tourist, and the horse-leading service was suspended while local authorities worked to improve market order.

Read more → How Flood Detention Basins Work as a 'Final Card' in Flood Control 中国新闻网社会

Henan activated the Nihewa flood detention area in Wuyang, Luohe, at 2 a.m. today to store excess floodwater from the Shaying River system and reduce the flood peak, according to the Ministry of Water Resources. The basin has a designed storage capacity of 238 million cubic meters. Before activation, all residents who needed to be relocated from the area had been safely evacuated. The article also explains the role of flood detention areas as a final line of defense in flood control.
