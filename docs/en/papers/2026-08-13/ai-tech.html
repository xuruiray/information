MIDAS: Mutual Information Disentanglement with Uncertainty-Aware Fusion for Incomplete Multimodal Sentiment Analysis arXiv 人工智能

Most multimodal sentiment analysis approaches assume access to complete multimodal inputs, but real-world applications frequently encounter incomplete or corrupted modalities. MIDAS introduces mutual information disentanglement with uncertainty-aware fusion to handle such incomplete inputs, preserving robust sentiment classification performance even when parts of the signal are missing.

Read more → LLM Agents Factory: Retrieval of Domain-Specific LLM Agents arXiv 计算语言学

Large language model agents are often designed on the fly for each request, which is computationally expensive and unstable. The LLM Agents Factory framework retrieves domain-specific agents from a prebuilt library, reducing overhead and improving task reliability. This approach enables efficient reuse of agents in production settings.

Read more → TAF-MED: Multi-Turn Safety Refusal Collapse in LLMs Under Declared Self-Treatment Intent arXiv 计算语言学

Large language models increasingly provide conversational health information that may influence treatment decisions. TAF-MED, a physician-reviewed benchmark, tests whether medication-safety boundaries persist across follow-up turns after explicit self-treatment intent. The findings reveal safety refusal collapse, highlighting risks in multi-turn health dialogues. This underscores the need for stronger safeguards in medical chatbots.

Read more → From assistance to execution: How enterprises put AI to work OpenAI 新闻

OpenAI's latest research reveals that enterprises are shifting from AI-assisted work to agentic execution. Using tools like ChatGPT and Codex, businesses are integrating AI into their operations, with frontier firms pulling ahead in adoption. Read more → When Chain-of-Thought Helps and When It Hurts: An Empirical Investigation of the Serial-Depth Bottleneck in LLM Reasoning arXiv 计算语言学

It is widely assumed that chain-of-thought prompting universally improves LLM reasoning. This empirical study, grounded in a bandwidth constraint framework, shows that when serial depth is limited, chain-of-thought can actually harm performance. The results warn against relying on chain-of-thought without considering model constraints. Practitioners should evaluate when to enable it.

Research & Governance

The Research & Governance subsection covers model safety and robustness. This edition features GFlowNet-based adversarial attack generation for exposing LLM vulnerabilities, an uncertainty-aware ensemble method that improves classification robustness under noisy data, and the CASE framework, which provides a multi-disciplinary control architecture for governing enterprise agentic AI. Together, these works support more trustworthy and accountable AI systems.

Generating Attacks for LLMs with GFlowNets arXiv 人工智能

As large language models are widely integrated into applications, security vulnerabilities become a critical concern. This research uses GFlowNets to generate adversarial attack samples, automatically uncovering model flaws. The method provides a new tool for identifying and mitigating malicious inputs before deployment, complementing existing safety evaluation techniques.

Read more → Uncertainty-Aware Ensemble Deep Randomized Neural Networks for Classification arXiv 机器学习

Deep randomized neural networks often treat all training samples uniformly, which limits their robustness on noisy real-world data. This paper proposes an uncertainty-aware ensemble method that adaptively weights samples based on uncertainty, improving classification robustness and generalization. This approach offers a more reliable alternative for classification tasks with noisy data.

Read more → The CASE Framework: A Multi-Disciplinary Control Architecture for Governing Enterprise Agentic AI arXiv 人工智能

Enterprises are deploying autonomous AI agents faster than they can govern them. The CASE framework argues that agentic AI governance is four distinct problems, each with mature control disciplines. By integrating DevSecOps and other approaches, it offers a unified architecture for enterprise accountability and safety.
