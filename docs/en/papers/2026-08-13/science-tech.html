Scientific Research Briefing

The science research brief covers breakthroughs in biomaterials, quantum physics, neuroscience, and atmospheric science: wool-derived keratin supports bone regrowth, electron crystals reveal hidden motion, an Ozempic-related study locates a craving center, and Starlink satellites map the upper atmosphere.

Scientists turn sheep’s wool into a material that helps regrow bone ScienceDaily 科学

Keratin extracted from sheep’s wool helped damaged bone regenerate in animals, producing tissue more organized and structurally similar to healthy bone than conventional collagen scaffolds. The discovery could turn an abundant agricultural byproduct into a promising material for regenerative medicine.

Physicists probed the elusive Wigner crystal, a quantum state where electrons organize into a crystal-like pattern, by shining light on an atomically thin material cooled near absolute zero. Optical signals revealed hidden electron motion.

Read more → Ozempic may have revealed the brain’s hidden “craving center” ScienceDaily 科学

Ozempic-like drugs may reduce cravings for alcohol and other addictive substances, not just hunger. Scientists trace the effect to the lateral septum, a brain region rich in GLP-1 receptors that links memories and surroundings with reward, potentially a key control point for addiction.
