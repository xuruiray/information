Lead Story
China launches nationwide campaign to study Ecological Environment Code

The Publicity Department of the CPC Central Committee, together with 19 other agencies, has launched a campaign to study and promote the new Ecological Environment Code of the People's Republic of China. Local authorities are urged to use Aug. 15, National Ecology Day and the code's effective date, to organize public outreach and themed events, enhance ecological civilization legal awareness, and practice the philosophy that 'lucid waters and lush mountains are invaluable assets.' The campaign aims to foster broad participation in building a modernization where humanity and nature coexist harmoniously.
