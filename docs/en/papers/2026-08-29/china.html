China Moves to Create 'Legal Lane' for Autonomous Driving 中国新闻网时政

China's road traffic safety law is undergoing its first systematic overhaul. The draft revision adds a special chapter on autonomous vehicles, setting out legal boundaries and regulatory frameworks for self-driving cars. This move is seen as a crucial step to support the rapid development of autonomous driving technology while ensuring road safety, and has attracted significant public attention.

100-Day Countdown to 16th China International Aviation & Aerospace Exhibition Kicks Off in Zhuhai 中国新闻网社会

The 100-day countdown ceremony for the 16th China International Aviation & Aerospace Exhibition and the "World Airshow Dialogue" were held on Aug 28 in Zhuhai, Guangdong. Local governments and leading enterprises gathered to discuss new opportunities in global aerospace and smart manufacturing.

Read more → Science Museums Nationwide Received Over 30 Million Visitors During Summer 2026 中国新闻网社会

According to the China Science and Technology Museum on Aug 28, during the summer of 2026, science museums nationwide received over 30 million visitors and held more than 100,000 educational and exhibition experiences. Science popularization is shifting from "touring exhibitions" to "immersive exploration."

Read more → Experts Decode Housing Sales System Reform: Making What You See What You Get 中国新闻网社会

The Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development, the Ministry of Natural Resources, and the National Financial Regulatory Administration recently issued a notice on improving the commercial housing sales system. Experts say the reform responds to major changes in housing supply and demand, and will accelerate a new real estate development model.

Read more → CCTV Reporter Visits Resettlement Site in Gyirong Town, Tibet, Supplies Ensure Basic Living 中国新闻网社会

On Aug 26, a mudslide triggered from the Nepalese side caused heavy casualties and missing persons at Gyirong Port in Shigatse, Tibet. As of 1 a.m. Aug 29, two people had been rescued, with 7 confirmed dead and 554 missing. A CCTV reporter visited the resettlement site in Gyirong Town, where supplies have arrived to ensure basic living.
