Frontier Models: From Clinical Explanations to Automated Design

This subsection covers frontier model research and industry developments: a feasibility study showing how standalone LLMs and pre-specified agentic pipelines can explain ICU mortality predictions; PICasso, an AI-enabled framework for autonomous silicon photonic device design; CIFQA, a deterministic tool-grounded multi-agent LLM framework for financial query answering; and an evaluation of LLM-generated literature reviews under short vs. long contexts. In industry, OpenAI decided to wind down its contract providing models to Cursor after SpaceX's acquisition.

Standalone LLM and a Pre-specified Agentic Pipeline for Explaining ICU Mortality Predictions: a Feasibility Study on the eICU Demo Dataset arXiv 人工智能

Machine-learning models predict ICU mortality well, yet feature-attribution alone often fails to provide the clinical narrative needed at the bedside. This study investigates whether a standalone LLM and a pre-specified agentic pipeline can bridge that gap, conducting a feasibility analysis on the eICU demo dataset.

Read more → PICasso: An AI-Enabled Design Framework for Autonomous Optimization of Silicon Photonic Devices arXiv 人工智能

PICasso is an AI-assisted framework for automated synthesis, verification, and optimization of photonic integrated circuits (PICs) from natural-language specifications. It couples a structured NL-to-YAML-to-GDS pipeline with PDK-aware knowledge injection and automated placement and routing, potentially lowering the barrier to photonic chip design.

Read more → CIFQA: A Deterministic Tool-Grounded Multi-Agent LLM Framework for Financial Query Answering arXiv 人工智能

Calculation-intensive financial QA demands exact reasoning over structured rates, temporal conditions, formulas, and constraints. CIFQA proposes a deterministic tool-grounded multi-agent LLM framework to address the common issue of LLMs producing numerically plausible but incorrect answers, improving reliability in finance.

Read more → Leveraging Large Language Models for Systematic Literature Review of Disease Spread Models arXiv 人工智能

This study develops an LLM pipeline to extract model-relevant information from 536 peer-reviewed papers on disease spread, exploring the feasibility of automating systematic literature reviews. Results suggest LLMs can significantly accelerate research synthesis and summarization.

Research & Governance: Interpretability and Data Frameworks

This subsection highlights methodological advances: a review of large models for battery prognostics and health management outlining future research directions, and a proposed unified framework for 5D multi-table analysis that addresses complexity in relational data across healthcare and other domains.

Large Models for Battery Prognostics and Health Management: A Review and Future Roadmap arXiv 人工智能

This review addresses Battery Prognostics and Health Management (BPHM), noting that conventional physics-based and task-centric deep learning approaches face challenges. It systematically examines the potential of large models for battery state assessment and lifetime prediction, and outlines a future research roadmap for EVs and grid storage.

Read more → Methodological and Conceptual Framework for 5D Multi-Table Analysis: A Unified Approach for Complex Data Reuse arXiv 人工智能

Multi-table learning remains a key challenge in healthcare and other complex systems. This work proposes a 5D unified framework for multi-table data analysis, integrating data volume, high-dimensional variables, high-cardinality features, and inter-table dependencies, aiming to improve data reuse and analysis efficiency in AI applications.
