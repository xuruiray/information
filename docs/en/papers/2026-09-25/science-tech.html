Distant time crystals can somehow fall into the same rhythm ScienceDaily 科学

Researchers have shown that multiple time crystals inside a semiconductor can synchronize their oscillations, much like pendulum clocks. The coupling is carried by spin-polarized electrons, allowing time crystals separated by up to 40 micrometers to lock to a common frequency, revealing unexpectedly long-range interactions.
