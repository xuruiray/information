Lead Story
Scientists turn DNA into a memory device that uses 100x less power

Researchers combined synthetic DNA with a semiconductor to create an ultra-low-power memory device capable of storing and processing information in the same place. This bio-hybrid technology, described as a potential game-changer, could eventually help make AI systems and next-generation computers far more energy efficient, addressing a key bottleneck in computing performance and sustainability.
