The material first entered a fleeting electronic state in which its bonds reorganized in a repeating pattern, followed by tiny atomic shifts. This ultrafast pathway could offer a new way to control electronic properties.

Scientific Research

Research highlights span nuclear physics, biomedicine, quantum physics, and archaeological materials. Hidden magnetism inside atoms may explain mysterious gamma rays ScienceDaily 科学

For decades, scientists puzzled over why certain atomic nuclei unexpectedly emit large numbers of low-energy gamma rays. A new experiment traces the effect to magnetic changes inside the nucleus, where protons and neutrons flip their tiny internal magnets. The discovery could sharpen models of nuclear reactions and related physics.

Read more → This weight-loss hormone may protect the liver even without weight loss ScienceDaily 科学

A hormone already known for curbing appetite and aiding weight loss may have a surprising second role: protecting the liver from inflammation and scarring. Researchers at McMaster University found that GDF15 activates a previously unknown brain-to-liver pathway, triggering glucocorticoid release and calming immune activity in the liver.
