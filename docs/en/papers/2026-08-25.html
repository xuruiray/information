Lead Story
China Moves to Establish Road Safety Rules for Self-Driving Cars

The draft amendment to China's Road Traffic Safety Law was submitted on Aug. 25 to the 24th session of the 14th NPC Standing Committee. It proposes a legal framework for autonomous driving road safety, measures against distracted driving and unauthorized road-use group activities, and raises the e-bike speed limit in non-motorized lanes from 15 to 20 km/h. It also mandates helmets for e-bike riders and passengers.
