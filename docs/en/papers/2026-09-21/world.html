Michelle Bachelet withdraws from race to become UN secretary general 卫报国际

Former Chilean President Michelle Bachelet has withdrawn her candidacy for the next UN secretary general after struggling to gain traction among Security Council members. The 74-year-old’s exit leaves seven candidates in the race. Her campaign had failed to secure sufficient support in a straw poll.

German Chancellor Friedrich Merz called state elections in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern and Berlin a "disaster" for his centre-right CDU party. Exit polls show damaging losses, but Merz vows to stay on. The results reflect growing dissatisfaction with the ruling coalition.
