Fire at Textile Factory in Shunde, Guangdong Kills 8 中国新闻网社会

A fire broke out on the afternoon of September 20 at a textile company's plant in Shunde district, Foshan, Guangdong province, killing eight people and injuring one. Search and rescue efforts continued, and the cause of the blaze is under investigation.

Read more → Poyang Lake Level Falls Below 12 Meters for First Time This Year, Entering Dry Season 中国新闻网社会

Affected by weather conditions and reduced upstream inflows, Poyang Lake's water level kept falling. At 4 p.m. on September 20, the Xingzi hydrological station recorded 11.99 meters, slipping below the 12-meter dry-season mark for the first time this year, and by 10 a.m. on September 21 it had dropped to 11.67 meters, with lake area and volume both smaller than a year earlier.
