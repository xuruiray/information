Funding & M&A Briefing

The Funding & M&A subsection includes one story. TechCrunch reports a small but growing number of founders betting that bringing people together offline is its own industry. These founders believe technology has eroded real human connection over the past two decades, and that rebuilding it through events, spaces and services presents a business opportunity. The report does not disclose specific funding amounts or deal details.

A Growing Number of Founders Are Betting on Offline Connection as Its Own Industry TechCrunch 创业

TechCrunch reports that a small but growing number of founders are betting offline human connection can be its own industry. Using a game console and a leathercraft school as examples, the report notes that seemingly unrelated businesses both see value in fostering the kind of connection technology has eroded over two decades. No specific funding or M&A deals were disclosed.
