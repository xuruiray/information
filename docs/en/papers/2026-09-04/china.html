China Watch Briefing

This edition of China Watch is anchored by medical-insurance reform and emergency response. China's NHSA said labor analgesia is now fully reimbursable and that fund supervision will pursue seven types of 'full coverage.' It is also making services nearby, smart and direct, with long-term care insurance expected to basically cover the whole country by the end of 2028. In Tibet, fuel supplies are being mobilized to support rescue efforts after a debris flow; Fujian and Jiangxi face heavy-rain risks. Elsewhere, a Jinan Start-up Area official is under investigation, and a fact-checking campaign in Qinghai is addressing online rumors about goji berries and camel milk.

Society & Law Briefing

This edition looks at public services and safety: medical insurance services are set to become more convenient, with long-term care insurance expected to basically cover the whole country by the end of 2028. Heavy rain from the remnant of Typhoon Sader continues to pose risks in Fujian and Jiangxi, while a fact-checking campaign in Qinghai takes on online claims about goji berries and camel milk.

NHSA: Make Medical Insurance Services 'Nearby, Smart and Direct' 中国新闻网社会

The National Healthcare Security Administration said at a press conference that it will promote 'nearby handling, smart handling and direct handling' for medical insurance services. The goal is to simplify procedures and improve access, reducing the time and burden for insured people to go through administrative processes. The plan is part of broader service-quality improvements.

Read more → NHSA: Long-Term Care Insurance to Basically Cover All of China by End-2028 中国新闻网社会

At a State Council Information Office briefing on the high-quality development of medical insurance, NHSA Deputy Administrator Wang Wenjun said that by the end of 2028 the long-term care insurance system will basically achieve nationwide coverage. The effort will expand care protection for more disabled older adults and help ease pressure on families providing long-term care.

Read more → Typhoon Sader Remnants to Bring More Heavy Rain to Fujian, Jiangxi 中国新闻网社会

The 18th typhoon of the year, Sader, combined with cold air to produce strong rainfall on September 3, setting record-breaking torrential rain in parts of Fujian. Although Sader stopped being numbered in the evening, its remnant circulation will still bring heavy rain to Fujian, Jiangxi and surrounding areas on September 4, with secondary disasters a concern.

Read more → Fact-Check Campaign in Qinghai Tackles Rumors on Goji Berries, Camel Milk 中国新闻网社会

On September 3, a national 'rumor buster' fact-finding trip visited a rural revitalization industrial park in Ulan County, Haixi Prefecture, Qinghai, to check online claims that goji berry farming damages native saxaul forests and grasslands or that camel milk can cure diabetes, using on-site visits to reveal the true picture of local industries.
