Meta details ZGateway, a proxy positioned in front of ZippyDB, its most widely used key-value store, unifying traffic and adding admission control, load balancing and cross-region resilience. inclusionAI's Ling 3.0 Flash Sante, a health/medicine-focused version of Ling 3.0 Flash, is live on Vercel AI Gateway with free usage through October 4. The Mixture-of-Experts model has 124B total parameters with ~5.1B active per token, a 256K context window, and function calling, designed for medical reasoning.
