Government & Policy Briefing

UN votes to adopt new world map that shows Africa’s true scale 卫报国际

The United Nations voted to adopt a Togo-sponsored resolution formally phasing out the traditional Mercator world map in favour of a projection that more accurately shows Africa’s size. The decision responds to criticism that old maps profoundly distort the continent’s proportions, and could reshape educational and public maps around the world.

Read more → South Korea pushes back on reports it is readying Hormuz deployment amid US pressure 卫报国际

South Korea’s presidential office downplayed reports that Seoul is preparing to deploy military assets to the Strait of Hormuz and seeking parliamentary approval. Officials said no decision has been made, but the reports highlight growing US pressure on South Korea to contribute more to Gulf security amid tensions over shipping.

Read more → ‘It cost me many friendships’: former Brazil military chief reveals inside struggle to stop Bolsonaro’s coup 卫报国际

In his new book, former Brazilian military commander Carlos de Almeida Baptista Júnior describes the tense 2022 meeting when President Jair Bolsonaro, then refusing to accept his election loss, pressed armed-forces leaders to support a coup.
