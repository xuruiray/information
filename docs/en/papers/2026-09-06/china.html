Policy & Regulation: Defense, Diplomacy and Governance Updates

Policy & Regulation covers official defense, diplomacy and emergency measures: China launched the national defense education program 'United in Building National Defense'; the air force will send multiple aircraft types to Egypt Airshow for deeper military cooperation; the water ministry reported several rivers above alert levels and the Ganjiang flood crest; and the Asia-Pacific Media High-Level Forum in Shenzhen raised expectations for APEC cooperation.

Media program 'United in Building National Defense' debuts with immersive displays for National Defense Education Month 中国新闻网时政

China's Publicity Department and the Central Military Commission's Political Work Bureau, together with China Media Group, have launched the integrated media program 'United in Building National Defense' for National Defense Education Month. It uses immersive performances, interviews, cross-time dialogues and AI-generated content to explore new ways of defense education, airing on CCTV-12 on September 6.

Read more → Chinese Air Force aircraft to join Egypt Airshow 2026 中国新闻网时政

China's Ministry of National Defense said September 6 that, at the invitation of the Egyptian Armed Forces, the PLA Air Force will send fighter jets, early warning aircraft, tankers, helicopters and other aircraft to the 2nd Egypt Airshow at Alamein International Airport from September 8 to 10. It marks China's second participation, aimed at enhancing mutual trust and military cooperation.

Read more → Ministry of Water Resources: 10 rivers still above alert; Ganjiang flood crest passes Zhangshu 中国新闻网时政

China's Ministry of Water Resources held an emergency meeting on September 6 morning. At 11:00 a.m., ten rivers in Zhejiang, Jiangxi, Heilongjiang and elsewhere remained above warning levels, with a maximum surplus of 1.04 meters. The No.1 flood crest on Jiangxi's Ganjiang River has passed Zhangshu, and the main stream is expected to fall below warning levels within 24 hours.

The Asia-Pacific Media High-Level Forum opened in Shenzhen on September 5, drawing more than 400 delegates from over 40 countries and regions, UN agencies and international organizations. As part of APEC's China Year events, the forum gave participants a window into China's development and a platform to voice expectations for regional cooperation and shared prosperity.

Society & Law: Rescue Operations and Security Alerts

Society & Law coverage centers on disasters and public security. At Gyirong port, soldiers and local residents support each other amid search and rescue operations. A mudslide in Suichuan, Jiangxi, has killed two and left ten missing, with rescue ongoing. Elsewhere, an attempt to install malware on a computer at a courier station shows the need to report threats to state security via the hotline.

After 11 years, he returns to rescue frontline in Gyirong 中国新闻网时政

At the search-and-rescue site near Gyirong port, armed police were digging through mud and rocks. A soldier who had taken part in a rescue in the same area 11 years ago has returned, racing against time with his comrades to find missing people, embodying the commitment of Chinese soldiers in times of crisis.

This on-the-scene report captures how troops, local rescuers and affected residents support each other in the disaster area. From coordinated digging efforts to residents bringing hot meals to rescue workers, the warmth of solidarity prevails amid the ruins.

Read more → Mudslide in Jiangxi's Suichuan kills 2, leaves 10 missing 中国新闻网时政

Fire rescuers in Jiangxi found one missing person without vital signs on the morning of September 6 in Mingkeng Village, Gaoping Town, Suichuan County, according to the Nanchang fire rescue detachment. The mudslide and landslide disaster has now killed two people, with ten still missing. Rescue operations continue.

Read more → Spy caught trying to steal data at courier station; employee reports to state security hotline 中国新闻网时政

In a suspected espionage attempt, an intruder climbed into a courier station before dawn, bypassed cameras and inserted a USB drive preloaded with malware into an office computer. A staff member on duty spotted the act and called the 12339 state security hotline, prompting a timely response. The case underscores the need for public vigilance against espionage.
