Oracle bans AI-generated code from OpenJDK Hacker News

Oracle has banned AI-generated code from OpenJDK, a policy that appears to contradict founder Larry Ellison's earlier remark that Oracle is not writing its own code. The decision has drawn sharp reactions from developers, who debate whether AI-generated contributions should be treated differently from human-written code, and what it means for open-source governance and licensing. The discussion reflects a broader uncertainty about AI's role in software maintenance and community trust.

A site operator shares a year-long battle against scrapers, revealing that 99% of traffic to a 1.5 million-page website is bots. The post details the escalating arms race between automated crawlers and defensive measures, from rate limiting to fingerprinting. It raises important questions about content ownership, fair use, and the future of the open web as AI companies harvest data at scale. The author's frustration resonates with many webmasters and sparks a heated community thread on how to protect independent sites.
