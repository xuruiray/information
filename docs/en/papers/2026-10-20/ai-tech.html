This edition covers frontier models, AI products and platforms, and research and governance. MiniMax M3.1 Flash is free for a limited time on Vercel AI Gateway; Nvidia released open models for enterprise and PC agents; Inception's Mercury 3 uses diffusion for high-speed text generation.
