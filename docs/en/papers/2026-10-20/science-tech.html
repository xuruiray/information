Science research: a new magnetism form, Alzheimer's walking link and optogenetics Nobel

Four research developments: the discovery of altermagnetism won a major physics prize, challenging longstanding physics; about 3,000 daily steps were linked to slower Alzheimer's-related brain changes, less harmful tau buildup and nearly half the cognitive and functional decline, with benefits appearing strongest at 5,000 steps; optogenetics won the 2026 Nobel Prize, offering possibilities for activating memories, restoring vision and treating brain disorders; and a highly excited but orderly fractional Fermi sea was created in ultracold atoms, challenging established quantum theory and hinting at exotic quasiparticles.
