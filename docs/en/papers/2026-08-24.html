How Ora benchmarks every major AI agent on Vercel

Vercel's blog explains how Ora benchmarks every major AI agent on live websites, instructing agents to sign up for products, integrate, and pay. With a 16-person engineering team producing hundreds of commits a day, Ora runs agents side-by-side on real sites and often sees them fail. By Ora's estimate, 99% of the web isn't agent-ready. The piece also highlights the benefits of having front-end, back-end, and agent runtime on one platform. This real-world testing offers practical insight into AI agent reliability.
