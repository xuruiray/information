Panama canal traffic to be cut again as drought from El Niño worsens 卫报国际

The Panama Canal plans to cut maritime traffic again because of a worsening drought linked to El Niño, further restricting one of the world’s most important shipping lanes. The canal handles about 5% of global maritime trade, and the reductions could add pressure to global logistics and supply chains.
