Frontier Models

5 articles
Frontier Models Briefing

Frontier model research focuses on temporal compositional reasoning, long-horizon agent failure attribution, and benchmarking LLMs against classical methods such as Naive Bayes. Perplexity adopts GPT-6 Astra for end-to-end systems, signaling that frontier models are taking on production tasks, while ZGCM-1 explores data, system, and algorithmic efficiency in a fully open 7B model.

TimeThink: Eliciting Compositional Reasoning in Timeseries Large Language Models arXiv 人工智能

The paper notes that timeseries multimodal large language models often fail to capture dynamic temporal patterns, offering only implicit reasoning that lacks explanation. It introduces TimeThink, an approach that elicits compositional reasoning so that models explicitly use temporal structure when answering questions over time series data.

Read more → Root-Cause Attribution Is a Search Problem: Continual Search for Long-Horizon Agent Failures arXiv 人工智能

As AI agents take on long-horizon tasks, execution logs grow too large for human review. The paper frames root-cause attribution as a search problem and proposes continual search to turn outcome-level signals into actionable interventions, improving agent reliability.

Read more → LLMs or Naive Bayes? Old Gems or New Ways arXiv 机器学习

The paper revisits whether classical methods should be retired, benchmarking Complement Naive Bayes against zero-shot and few-shot LLMs across four model families and a 37x scale range, from 27B models to a 1T-parameter mixture-of-experts. It offers a broad empirical comparison of old and new approaches.

Read more → Perplexity trusts GPT-6 Astra with end-to-end systems OpenAI 新闻

Perplexity uses Astra to write communications, modify software, and monitor production systems, checking its work far less often than with earlier models. The case shows frontier models moving deeper into end-to-end production workflows, highlighting growing trust in model autonomy.

Read more → ZGCM-1: A Fully Open and Extremely Efficient Foundation Model for Math and Agentic Search arXiv 人工智能

The work presents ZGCM-1, a fully open 7B dense foundation model trained from scratch with emphasis on data, system, and algorithmic efficiency. The team argues compact models cannot passively memorize the open web but can overcome capacity limits through deliberate interaction, performing well on math and agentic search.

Research & Governance

5 articles
Research & Governance Briefing

Research and governance coverage spans model safety, evaluation reliability, and knowledge transfer. Machine unlearning for speech QA, layer-wise harmfulness propagation, hindsight bias in clinical temporal reasoning, and a multimodal document reasoning benchmark all point to trustworthy evaluation and risk mitigation, while knowledge distillation work focuses on preserving category correlation knowledge.

Machine Unlearning for Speech Question Answering in Large Audio-Language Models arXiv 机器学习

Large audio-language models show strong speech understanding and question answering but inherit privacy risks from training data, including unintended memorization of sensitive information. The paper studies machine unlearning for speech QA, aiming to remove specific knowledge while preserving model capability.

Read more → TestHallVQA: Exploring LVLMs' Document-Level Reasoning under Redundant Contexts from Scientific Exams arXiv 计算语言学

Existing planar visual question answering benchmarks tend to stress isolated challenges, either long-document understanding with shallow reasoning or complex visual reasoning alone. The paper introduces TestHallVQA, using scientific exam materials with redundant context to probe document-level reasoning and robustness in large vision-language models.

Read more → Discovering and Preserving Category Correlation Knowledge via Adaptive Reciprocal Knowledge Distillation arXiv 机器学习

Knowledge distillation helps lightweight student models learn from larger teachers, yet a substantial size gap often impedes transfer, and most methods use static, one-way strategies. The paper proposes adaptive reciprocal knowledge distillation to discover and preserve category correlation knowledge, improving lightweight student performance.

Read more → Harmfulness Propagation Dynamics: Layer-wise Trajectories of Adversarial Intent in Large Language Models arXiv 计算语言学

The paper identifies Harmfulness Propagation Dynamics: for harmful prompts, the projection of the last-token hidden state onto a learned harm direction rises monotonically with transformer depth, while benign prompts remain flat or oscillatory. This cross-layer signature offers a new lens on harmful intent.

Read more → Hindsight Bias in Clinical Temporal Reasoning: How Future Data Exposure Affects Large Language Model Judgment arXiv 计算语言学

Clinical decisions are prospective, yet clinical language models are often evaluated on retrospective records that reveal final diagnoses, treatment responses, and outcomes. Such evaluations may reward the use of future information rather than reasoning under uncertainty at the decision point. The paper analyzes this hindsight bias and its effects.
