US promises $1bn in security aid to Colombia as new rightwing president pledges drugs crackdown 卫报国际

The Trump administration has announced plans to provide $1 billion in security assistance to Colombia's new right-wing government, led by Abelardo De La Espriella, who was sworn in and used his inaugural speech to vow a 'profound transformation' of the country's destiny, including a crackdown on drugs. The aid signals a major U.S. commitment to the new administration's security agenda.

Read more → Judge approves Trump effort to end South Sudan TPS protections 卫报国际

A U.S. federal judge has approved the Trump administration's effort to end temporary protected status (TPS) for hundreds of South Sudanese nationals in the United States. The ruling follows a Supreme Court decision that allowed the government to strip similar protections for Haitians and Syrians. The move could affect many families who fled conflict, though legal challenges may continue.

Read more → Canadian government steps in as 'out of control' wildfires force thousands to flee 卫报国际

Canada's federal government has announced urgent assistance for British Columbia, where out-of-control wildfires have forced about 22,000 people to flee their homes. A total of 102 fires are burning day and night, with their breakneck speed hampering firefighters' efforts. The government intervention comes as one of the most severe fire seasons in the province's history.

This section features Typhoon Dolphin's landfall in China and record heat in Hong Kong, along with a solo Arctic Ocean voyage, exploring human endurance and climate volatility.

Typhoon Dolphin makes landfall in China as its strongest storm this year 卫报国际

Typhoon Dolphin, the strongest tropical cyclone to hit China this year, has made landfall on the east coast, bringing torrential rain and strong winds and triggering warnings of flooding and landslides. The storm previously lashed Japan's southern Okinawa province before moving toward China, causing widespread disruption.

Read more → Hong Kong records hottest day ever as Typhoon Dolphin fuels heatwave 卫报国际

Hong Kong has recorded its highest official temperature since records began 142 years ago, posting 36.9 degrees Celsius on Sunday. The city's weather observatory attributed the intense heat to Typhoon Dolphin, which swept warm air over the region. The record heat highlights the growing impact of tropical storms on local climates amid global warming.

Read more → Meet the man circumnavigating the Arctic Ocean, alone NPR 国际

Matt Rutherford is attempting a world-first solo circumnavigation of the Arctic Ocean, sailing 10,000 nautical miles through ice floes and gales. Speaking from the cabin of his boat, he described the challenges of navigating treacherous waters in one of the most remote regions on Earth. The voyage underscores the extremes of human endurance and the preservation of Arctic waters.
