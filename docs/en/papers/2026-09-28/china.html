Former BAIC Chairman Xu Heyi Given Suspended Death Sentence in First Instance 中国新闻网时政

On September 28, the Beijing No. 3 Intermediate People's Court publicly sentenced Xu Heyi, former party secretary and chairman of Beijing Automotive Group, for bribery and money laundering. Xu received a death sentence with a two-year reprieve for bribery and two years in prison for money laundering; the combined punishment is a suspended death sentence, lifetime deprivation of political rights, and confiscation of all personal property.

Read more → Strong Cold Front Brings Wind, Rain and Snow to China, Local Temperatures Drop 10°C 中国新闻网社会

The China Meteorological Administration said on September 28 that a strong cold front will move southward through regions north of the Yangtze River and Jiangnan at the end of September, bringing local temperature drops of 10°C along with strong winds, rain and snow. Precautions are needed against impacts on transport and agriculture.

Chongqing Railway Station announced on September 28 that it sent a total of 708,000 passengers during the Mid-Autumn holiday from September 25 to 27. Passenger flow peaked on the first day, September 25, with 288,000 passengers, reflecting strong holiday travel demand.
