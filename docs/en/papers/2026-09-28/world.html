Embattled Serbian president resigns, paving way for early elections BBC 国际

Serbia's President Aleksandar Vucic has resigned, paving the way for early elections. His administration had been dogged by prolonged protests over corruption allegations. Vucic is expected to lead his ruling party into the snap parliamentary vote. The resignation marks a major political shift in the Balkan country, which has faced months of demonstrations. It remains unclear how the early elections will affect Serbia's domestic stability and its path toward European Union membership.

The move is seen as a gesture amid international pressure, and opposition supporters are demanding a clear timetable for a vote. The political situation remains tense as the country navigates a transition.
