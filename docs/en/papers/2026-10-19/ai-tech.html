Research and Governance: Hebrew Relation Extraction, Unified Evaluation, Human Control, and AI Welfare

HeRE builds a Hebrew relation-extraction benchmark via LLM-guided denoising of knowledge-graph alignments. AuditKIT combines benchmark accuracy, judge-based assessment, runtime performance, tool use, and retrieval quality into a unified evaluation spine. RealCLI introduces a paired benchmark for human-agent collaboration quality in longer-horizon CLI tasks. Structured authority injection uses typed MCP overrides so human operators’ binding directives persist across LLM agent planning cycles. Functional welfare argues AI welfare is also a safety issue and studies welfare-like model states that causally influence behavior.
