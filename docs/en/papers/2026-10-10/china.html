Policy & Regulation Briefing

At the State Council Information Office briefing on the 15th Five-Year Plan, officials reported 10.52 million new urban jobs in the first nine months and 1.079 billion basic pension enrollees. During the plan period, China aims to train 1 million high-level technical talents annually. A key document on developing new quality productive forces was also published, setting overall requirements and key tasks.

China to Train 1 Million High-Level and Urgently Needed Technical Talents Annually During 15th Five-Year Plan 中国新闻网时政

On Oct 10, the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security said that during the 15th Five-Year Plan, China will invest more in people, providing subsidized vocational skills training to 50 million person-times and helping 40 million obtain skill certificates, including 14 million at senior technician level or above. It will also train 1 million high-level and urgently needed professional technical talents annually.

Read more → China’s Basic Pension Insurance Covers 1.079 Billion People as of End-September 中国新闻网时政

Vice Minister Li Zhong said on Oct 10 that by the end of September, China’s basic pension, unemployment, and work injury insurance schemes covered 1.079 billion, 251 million, and 327 million people respectively. The three social insurance funds had a cumulative surplus of 11.2 trillion yuan.

Read more → China Adds 10.52 Million Urban Jobs in First Nine Months of 2026 中国新闻网时政

Vice Minister Li Zhong said on Oct 10 that China created 10.52 million urban jobs in the first nine months of 2026, completing 87.7% of the annual target. The average surveyed urban unemployment rate in the first eight months was 5.2%, indicating overall stable employment.

Read more → Xi Jinping’s Economic Thought Guides China to Accelerate Development of New Quality Productive Forces 中国新闻网时政

On Oct 9, the CPC Central Committee and the State Council publicly released the Opinions on Developing New Quality Productive Forces. The document clarifies the overall requirements and key tasks for China’s development of new quality productive forces and makes arrangements for strengthening organization and implementation.

Industry & Local Briefing

Industry and local news highlight regional development and infrastructure. Yibin is shifting from riverside factories to new-energy production lines. The Yangtze River Delta railway adjustment set record passenger and freight train numbers. Construction began on the Gezhouba shipping expansion, part of the Three Gorges waterway project. A new national standard for container rail-water intermodal transport aims to improve multimodal efficiency.

From Riverside Old Factories to New-Energy Production Lines: Yibin’s Industrial Transformation 中国新闻网社会

Yibin, known as the “first city on the Yangtze,” is shifting from traditional riverside factories to new-energy production lines, marking a significant industrial restructuring and epitomizing the green transformation of the Yangtze River Economic Belt. The shift reflects broader efforts to upgrade traditional industries and pursue sustainable growth along the river.

Read more → Yangtze River Delta Railway to Implement New Train Schedule from Tomorrow 中国新闻网社会

Starting at midnight on Oct 11, China’s railways will implement the fourth-quarter train schedule. After the adjustment, the Yangtze River Delta will operate 1,435.5 pairs of passenger trains and 1,383 pairs of freight trains, both record highs. The new schedule aims to better meet rising travel and logistics demand in the region.

Read more → Main Works of Gezhouba Shipping Expansion, Part of Three Gorges Waterway Project, Begin 中国新闻网时政

On Oct 10, construction of the upstream and downstream cofferdams for the Gezhouba shipping expansion, part of the Three Gorges waterway new channel project, officially began, marking the start of main works for the major water transport infrastructure project. The project is expected to boost shipping capacity and logistics efficiency on the Yangtze River.

Read more → New National Standard for Container Rail-Water Intermodal Loading and Safety Inspection Explained 中国新闻网社会

The Ministry of Transport has issued a recommended national standard, GB/T 48309—2026, for container rail-water intermodal loading and safety inspection. Effective Jan 1, 2027, it aims to unify loading and security requirements and improve multimodal transport efficiency. The standard is part of efforts to promote “one-order, one-container” multimodal services.

Society & Law Briefing

In society and law, the Supreme People's Court and the National Cultural Heritage Administration released six typical cases of cultural relic crimes, warning against imitating tomb-raiding novels or digging private graves. Media reported diners unknowingly becoming livestream backdrops, sparking privacy debates. Shanghai immigration authorities added night visa services after the National Day holiday, and the human resources ministry set a work injury insurance coverage target of 345 million for the 15th Five-Year Plan.

Imitating Tomb-Raiding Novels or Digging Graves in One’s Own Yard Is Criminal, Two Authorities Release Typical Cases 中国新闻网社会

The Supreme People’s Court and the National Cultural Heritage Administration jointly released six typical cases of cultural relic crimes, including organized tomb raiding imitating novel plots and digging ancient tombs on one’s own homestead. The cases warn that such crimes are becoming more professional, tech-savvy, and spreading online.

Read more → Dining Out, How Did I Become a Livestream Backdrop? 中国新闻网社会

Media reports highlight that restaurant diners in China have unknowingly become backdrops in online livestreams. Such incidents have sparked public debate over privacy rights and personal information protection in public commercial spaces, raising questions about consent, legal liability, and the boundaries of commercial filming for businesses.

Read more → Shanghai Immigration Authorities Open First Post-Holiday Night Visa Service Sessions 中国新闻网社会

Starting Oct 10, for two consecutive Saturdays, all 20 immigration service halls in Shanghai will simultaneously offer night visa application sessions to meet concentrated post-National Day demand. It is the first time such night services have been added after a long holiday to ease processing pressure.

Read more → China Aims to Cover 345 Million People with Work Injury Insurance in 15th Five-Year Plan 中国新闻网社会

Vice Minister Li Zhong said China aims to keep basic pension insurance coverage above 95%, expand unemployment insurance to 255 million people, and work injury insurance to 345 million during the 15th Five-Year Plan, promoting growth, structural optimization, and quality improvement. The targets are part of efforts to strengthen the social safety net.
