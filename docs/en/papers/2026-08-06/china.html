Supreme Court begins sweeping review of eco-environment judicial documents 中国新闻网时政

The Supreme People's Court announced on Aug 6 a centralized cleanup of 26 effective judicial interpretations, 35 judicial normative documents, and 45 guiding cases in the ecological environment field after the Eco-Environment Code took effect, in a bid to align judicial policies with the new code. The review covers all major normative instruments to ensure consistency and clarity in environmental adjudication across Chinese courts.

Read more → Supreme Court issues first judicial interpretation to support Eco-Environment Code 中国新闻网时政

The Supreme People's Court on Aug 6 released the 'Provisions on the Temporal Effect of the Application of the Eco-Environment Code of the People's Republic of China,' the first judicial interpretation supporting the code's implementation. It offers systematic guidance on how the code applies to cases arising before and after its effective date, helping courts handle legal transitions consistently.

Read more → Rizhao city official Wang Feng under investigation for discipline violations 中国新闻网时政

Wang Feng, deputy secretary of the Rizhao municipal Party committee and head of its political and legal affairs commission in Shandong Province, is under disciplinary and supervisory investigation for suspected serious disciplinary and legal violations, according to the provincial discipline watchdog on Aug 6. The announcement did not specify the allegations. The probe underscores the ongoing anti-corruption campaign targeting local officials.

Read more → China steadily advances data property rights registration 中国新闻网时政

China's National Data Administration said on Aug 6 that it is steadily advancing data property rights registration and accelerating the construction of a unified national data market, with the goal of providing institutional support for the circulation and use of data as a production factor. The move is part of broader efforts to formalize data ownership and facilitate market-based allocation.

Shanxi police seek public tips on gang crimes, including cyber violence 中国新闻网社会

The anti-gang office of the Shanxi Provincial Public Security Department issued a notice on Aug 6 soliciting public leads on gang-related crimes, explicitly covering new-type offenses such as cyber violence and coercive debt collection by criminal groups. The move responds to the rising prominence of illegal activities conducted online and aims to expand public participation in the crackdown.

Read more → Heilongjiang airports see busiest summer travel season on record 中国新闻网社会

Driven by cool summer weather and a packed calendar of cultural tourism events, air traffic in Heilongjiang Province has surged during the summer travel peak, with the provincial airport group reporting its busiest summer season ever. Officials attribute growth to events like the Harbin Summer Music Festival and the International Beer Festival, alongside a nationwide travel boom.

Read more → 200-billion-yuan ultra-long special treasury bonds allocated for aging community upgrades 中国新闻网社会

A 200-billion-yuan tranche of ultra-long special treasury bonds has been allocated to support livelihood projects in old residential communities, including elevator installation, providing financial backing for urban renewal and improved living conditions for residents. The funding is part of China's broader push to upgrade housing infrastructure and stimulate demand in the post-renovation economy.
