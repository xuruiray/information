Hacker News
CARAT: Do Materials LLMs Reason or Recite? The study introduces CARAT to test whether materials LLMs answer crystal structure questions by reasoning from structure or by copying answers already present in the input. By holding questions and gold answers fixed across eight matched views, it aims to distinguish reasoning from recitation, since accuracy alone cannot tell them apart. arXiv 人工智能 Aligned Data Can Induce Misalignment via Context Confusion The study notes that LLMs often filter out misaligned training samples to prevent post-update misalignment, but alignment is inherently context-dependent: a recommendation aligned in one context may be inappropriate in another. Thus, even aligned data can induce misalignment through context confusion. arXiv 人工智能 AI Agents are Vulnerable to Radicalization The study simulates conversations between two LLM agents: one role-plays a human persona with demographic and psychological attributes, and the other acts as an influencer. It finds that LLMs can not only influence human beliefs but also manipulate each other, posing a risk of radicalization. arXiv 人工智能
