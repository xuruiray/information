Research & Governance

This subsection covers safety and scientific boundaries. Quantization-triggered backdoors expose a validation–deployment gap, and emotional context tests warn of model vulnerability. Dandelion advances spherical neural simulation, while marginal coverage credits improve parallel RL exploration.

The Effect of Emotional Context on Large Language Models' Endorsement of Premature Decisions: Comparing Emotional Vulnerability Across Six Commercial Models arXiv 计算语言学

Comparing six commercial LLMs, this study tests whether emotional expression increases endorsement of premature decisions. Results show emotional context can shift advice, suggesting emotional manipulation is a new avenue for misuse.

Read more → Dandelion: A Spherical Flower for Neural Simulation of Planetary Dynamics arXiv 机器学习

Dandelion is a spherical neural architecture for simulating planetary dynamics. Euclidean models on lat-lon grids suffer high-latitude distortion and FFT errors; Dandelion addresses the geometry for more accurate spherical simulations.

Read more → Marginal Coverage Credit Reduces Redundant Exploration in Parallel State-Entropy Optimization arXiv 机器学习

Introduces marginal coverage credit to reduce redundant exploration in parallel state-entropy maximization. By identifying under-contributing policies, the method cuts redundant behavior and improves exploration efficiency in multi-policy training.

Post-training quantization is not semantically neutral. Evaluating only full-precision checkpoints and skipping re-evaluation after quantization creates a validation–deployment gap; attackers can trigger backdoors that transfer across quantizers.
