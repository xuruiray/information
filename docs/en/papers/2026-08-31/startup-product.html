New Products & Growth: AI Agent Tools Launch in Waves

AI-agent tools dominate this week's new product launches. Superagent offers a unified home for agents; oMLX accelerates Mac inference, cutting wait times from 90 seconds to five; Maritime provides dedicated agent computers for $1 per month; and Ulpaso challenges the high cost of meeting notes. Together, these products span management, performance, infrastructure, and application, signaling a maturing pipeline for AI agents.

oMLX Product Hunt

oMLX is a Mac LLM server that slashes AI agent wait times from 90 seconds to five seconds, dramatically improving local inference efficiency. For developers and agent applications that rely on large models, performance optimization is a critical pain point, and faster response times enable smoother interactions.

Read more → Maritime Product Hunt

Maritime offers dedicated computers for AI agents starting at $1 per month. The service provides isolated runtime environments that avoid interference and performance fluctuations from shared resources, lowering the barrier to deployment, especially for individual developers and small teams experimenting with or running agents.

Ulpaso focuses on meeting notes, arguing users should not pay a premium just to record meetings. It offers high-quality meeting summaries at a more affordable price, helping individuals and small businesses spend on core value rather than basic features.
