Five Localities Named for Illegal Land Occupation, Cropland Damage and Unlicensed Mining 中国新闻网社会

China's Ministry of Natural Resources and the National Forestry and Grassland Administration on August 31 publicized five typical violations found in the second quarter of 2026, including illegal land occupation, cropland damage, unlicensed mining and deforestation. The departments said the exposure aims to serve as a warning and push localities to rectify problems, safeguarding farmland and ecological red lines.

Read more → Xinjiang's Specialty Fruit Harvest Boosts Farmers' Income Through Industrial Upgrading 中国新闻网社会

As Xinjiang's specialty fruits enter peak market season, from Yengisar's plum orchards to Artux's fig processing bases, the region is leveraging an improved industrial chain to turn vibrant fruits into a 'fortune fruit' for local farmers, demonstrating the gains from industrial upgrading.

Read more → Rescuers Trek over Mountains into Gyirong, Explain Mission Challenges 中国新闻网社会

On August 29, four days after the Gyirong mudslide, a reporter joined a China Anneng rescue team in the hardest-hit area of Gyirong Town. With the final kilometers of G216 national road destroyed, crews worked to reopen the route while some rescuers trekked over mountains to reach the port, explaining the main challenges of the ongoing mission.

Organized by the provincial sports bureau and the Hangzhou government, the event includes VR esports, blending traditional mind sports with cutting-edge technology.
