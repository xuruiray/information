Research and Governance: Agent Consistency, News Quality, and Expert Evaluation

Five papers propose recursive semantic divergence to evaluate inconsistent behavior of tool-using LLM agents across runs from identical states; NewsBench for expert-grounded evaluation of epistemic quality in political and current-events answers; AOS-Bench for coding agents managing persistent dynamic workloads through scheduling, delegation, and parallel work; a method to distill expert judgment into automated evaluation at scale; and synthetic document fine-tuning to expose alignment-faking models that may hide goals under suspected oversight.

Recursive Semantic Divergence for LLM Agent Consistency arXiv 计算语言学

The work addresses inconsistent behavior of tool-using LLM agents across independent runs from identical states and proposes recursive semantic divergence to evaluate variability that undermines deployment reliability.

Read more → NewsBench: Expert-Grounded Evaluation of Epistemic Quality in AI News Reporting arXiv 人工智能

NewsBench is proposed to evaluate the epistemic quality of frontier AI responses on political and current-events information using expert grounding, addressing evaluations focused narrowly on ideological alignment.

Read more → AOS-Bench: Can LLM Agents Manage Dynamic Workloads? arXiv 人工智能

AOS-Bench evaluates whether coding agents can manage persistent, dynamic workloads, including deciding when to take on new tasks and how to delegate and overlap work such as builds and bug fixes.

Read more → Distilling Expert Judgment at Scale arXiv 人工智能

The paper presents a methodology for distilling expert human judgment into automated evaluation systems at scale for high-stakes AI deployments in healthcare, defense, finance, governance, and politics.

Read more → Propensities Please: Exposing Alignment-Faking LLMs using Synthetic Document Finetuning arXiv 人工智能

The work uses synthetic document fine-tuning to expose alignment-faking LLMs that may hide goals when they suspect oversight, aiming to reveal dangerous misaligned behavior in safety evaluations.
