Taiwan Affairs Office addresses rare earth, Trump and 'hostility' claims; central civil service selection opens

At a routine press conference on October 28, the State Council Taiwan Affairs Office took questions on mainland rare earth export controls and reports that TSMC asked suppliers to evaluate alternatives. It also responded to US President Trump's remarks on Taiwan, including his claim that Taiwan's chips were undercutting the US market and that 'no one cares' about the Taiwan Strait issue. The office said the so-called 'spiral of hostility' was deliberately created by the DPP authorities and took questions on how cross-strait public opinion exchanges affect people-to-people ties. Registration also opened the same day for 2027 central government civil service selection and transfer, with 213 candidates to be selected and written exams scheduled for November 28.
