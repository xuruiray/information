Baqing Earthquake Rescue Advances with Full Health and Epidemic Prevention Coverage

A 7.0-magnitude earthquake struck Baqing County in Nagqu, Tibet, at 22:33 on Oct. 22 at a depth of 10 km, causing major casualties. Medical rescue teams from several hospitals were dispatched overnight to treat the injured, and the National Disease Control Administration sent teams to organize public health risk assessments and said post-quake health and epidemic prevention coverage in the disaster area is now full. PetroChina set up a temporary fuel supply point at the epicenter on Oct. 23, and PLA troops immediately joined rescue and relief operations.
