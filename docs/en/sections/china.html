Policy and Regulation: Train Numbering Reform, Safety Supervision Rectification, Credit Standards Review

China Railway Group launched a nationwide train numbering reform, optimizing locomotive letter prefixes and number ranges in two batches in late November and December, and adding an 'X' prefix for Fuxing trains running at 350 km/h or above. The Ministry of Emergency Management said at its quarterly press conference that it will push for stricter safety supervision and law enforcement in response to problems exposed by several major accidents this year, adopting three sets of measures to resolutely rectify lax and weak enforcement; it also outlined 20 specific measures under a State Council General Office opinion to strengthen full-chain safety supervision of fireworks. The China National Institute of Standardization released the Report on the Credit Supervision Standards System for Market Entities (2026 Blue Book), systematically reviewing 1,038 publicly issued credit-related standards at various levels.
