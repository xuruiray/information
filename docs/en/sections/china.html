China Watch: CPC plenum opens as water, livelihood and public safety issues advance

The Fifth Plenary Session of the 20th CPC Central Committee opened in Beijing, with Xi Jinping delivering a work report and explaining a draft decision on full and rigorous Party governance. The water ministry set ecological flow targets for 382 cross-provincial rivers and lakes, while more than 90% of prefecture-level and above cities are advancing 15-minute community life circles. The Jianghan water diversion project is using nine machines simultaneously for tunnel construction; geological hazard checks were completed within 50 km of the Bachen earthquake epicenter; the meteorological administration refuted claims of a cyclone 'hitting the Himalayas'; and reports examined mandatory travel insurance and rescue pressures from inexperienced hikers entering wild mountains.
