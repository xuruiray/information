Authorities Warn Against Gold Social Security Card Scam Promising Higher Reimbursements and Subsidies 中国新闻网社会

A purported gold social security card has appeared online. Promoters claim it can raise medical reimbursement rates, secure pension subsidies and offer free cultural and tourism benefits. Some scammers impersonate human resources officials, using the pretext of issuing gold cards or subsidies to obtain personal information and induce transfers.

Read more → Chongqing Ends 2026 Flood Season After Wetter, Stronger-Than-Usual Conditions 中国新闻网社会

Chongqing ended its 2026 flood season at midnight on Oct. 10, the municipal water resources bureau said on Oct. 11. Rain and flood conditions in the city were generally heavier and stronger than usual this year. The announcement marks the close of the annual flood-control period.

Read more → Yangtze Decade: Former Fishers in Luzhou, Sichuan, Become River Guardians 中国新闻网社会

A Yangtze Decade feature focuses on former fishers in Luzhou, Sichuan, who have shifted to new livelihoods. Once fishermen, they have become river guardians, joining ecological protection efforts to keep the Yangtze's waters clean as it leaves Sichuan.

Read more → Luoyang Consensus on Urban Civilization Released in Henan 中国新闻网社会

The 2026 World Mayors Dialogue opened in the ancient capital of Luoyang, Henan, on Oct. 11, where the Luoyang Consensus on Urban Civilization was released. Participating city representatives reached a four-point consensus on people-centered development, preserving urban heritage, promoting green transition and advocating mutual learning among civilizations.
