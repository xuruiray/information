Beijing Police File Case Against Yixin-Related Companies; Actual Controllers Taken Under Criminal Coercive Measures 中国新闻网社会

The Chaoyang Branch of the Beijing Municipal Public Security Bureau has opened an investigation into suspected illegal and criminal activities by Yixin-related companies. Actual controllers Tang and Zhao and several other suspects have been subjected to criminal coercive measures. Investors are asked to register their cases with police.

Read more → Giant Panda Population in Sichuan Recovers Steadily; Umbrella Effect Emerges 中国新闻网社会

The Sichuan Provincial Forestry and Grassland Administration said on October 12 that over the past five years Sichuan has continued special surveys of giant pandas, snow leopards, golden snub-nosed monkeys and their habitats. Protection in the Giant Panda National Park has achieved notable results, habitat quality has improved, and biodiversity has steadily increased.

Read more → Don't Pick or Eat the 'Mini Apples' in Roadside Green Belts 中国新闻网社会

As ornamental trees bear fruit in late autumn, some residents in Yangzhou, Jiangsu and other places have been picking crabapples from green belts. Reporters warn that roadside fruit may be contaminated by pesticides and vehicle exhaust, posing food safety risks, and should not be picked or eaten.

Read more → American Friend Sakolsky to Reunite with Yin Yuzhen in Rome, Plans to Visit China in 2027 中国新闻网社会

American friend Sakolsky, whose real name is Ronald Sakolsky, is preparing to establish the Sakolsky Foundation and has opened WeChat accounts to share progress on exchange programs. He said he will reunite with Yin Yuzhen in Rome and plans to visit China again in 2027.
