China Watch: Railway sci-tech plan, vocational education funding growth, and local industry and legal developments

On October 12, the National Railway Administration issued the 15th Five-Year Plan for railway sci-tech innovation, incorporating railway sci-tech planning into the national sci-tech management system for the first time. The Ministry of Education disclosed a 23.3% increase in national vocational education funding during the 14th Five-Year Plan and briefed on the 2026 World Vocational and Technical Education Development Conference, which will release the Beijing Agreement. Locally, Jiangxi launched the 'New Long March' online theme campaign, the Three Gorges hub new channel test site improved efficiency through soil and wastewater recycling, and the 2026 World Mayor Dialogue in Luoyang concluded. In legal and social governance, former Guangxi Medical University president Zhao Jinmin was expelled from the CPC, former Jiangxi CPPCC vice chairman Hu Youtao was sentenced to 13 years for bribery, Chongqing published a food industry ban list with 255 lifetime bans, and Dalian police announced five typical cases including extortion of university teachers using 'copyright.'

Policy and Regulation: Railway 15th Five-Year sci-tech plan and vocational education funding growth

The National Railway Administration released the 15th Five-Year Plan for railway sci-tech innovation, setting goals, key tasks and safeguard measures and incorporating railway sci-tech planning into the national sci-tech management system for the first time. At a Ministry of Education press conference, officials disclosed that national vocational education funding grew 23.3% during the 14th Five-Year Plan and briefed on the 2026 World Vocational and Technical Education Development Conference and international cooperation. The conference will be held in Tianjin from October 15 to 17 and will release the Beijing Agreement, the first international standard in vocational education.
