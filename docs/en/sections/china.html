Guangxi Railway Hub Expansion Nears Completion; Two Chinese Irrigation Projects Join World Heritage List

In the early hours of Oct 14, track connection work at four points at Mahuang and Kangxiling stations in Qinzhou, Guangxi, was completed, concluding the four-stage station overhaul of Mahuang Station and pushing the Qinzhou-Fangchenggang double-tracking project into its final stage. On the evening of Oct 14 Beijing time, the International Commission on Irrigation and Drainage announced the 2026 World Heritage Irrigation Structures list: the Longjin ancient weir irrigation project in Changtai, Zhangzhou, Fujian, became the first in southern Fujian; the Taolai River oasis irrigation system in Gansu became the province's first, among four Chinese projects selected.

Former Chengdu Mayor Charged; Former Golog Deputy Governor Turns Himself In

The bribery case of former Chengdu mayor Wang Fengchao was concluded by the National Supervisory Commission and transferred to prosecutors; the Supreme People's Procuratorate approved his arrest and designated the Changsha procuratorate to review and prosecute, and public charges have been filed with the Changsha Intermediate People's Court. Xire, former Party member and deputy governor of Qinghai's Golog Prefecture government, turned himself in and is under disciplinary and supervisory investigation for suspected serious violations. Guangzhou Railway's Changsha passenger transport section reported that a man was administratively detained for defecating in a high-speed train seat. On the afternoon of Oct 14, a landslide hit a rural road from Huangbaiyuan Township to Hetaoping Village in Taibai County, Baoji, Shaanxi, disrupting traffic; local authorities are clearing it.
