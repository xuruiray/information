Society and Law: Post-Quake Transport, Resettlement and Emergency Rescue

After the magnitude-7.0 Baqing earthquake affected sections of the Qinghai-Tibet railway between Golmud and Lhasa, Lhasa railway station activated an emergency passenger plan and temporarily suspended or delayed relevant trains. Resettlement in the disaster area shifted from safe overnight shelter to warmer housing, with heating stoves distributed and prefabricated housing being built. Mashan county in Guangxi reported that several monkeys drowned in an agricultural irrigation tank, initially attributed to accidental drinking, and added escape routes after retrieving and disinfecting the site; a grandfather and granddaughter in Jinzhong, Shanxi, were rescued by firefighters after falling into a 10-meter-deep water tank when a cover collapsed.
