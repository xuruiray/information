China Watch: Industrial Upgrading and Export Pressure Coexist as Local Safety Governance Responds to Quake and Risks

This edition of China Watch covers industry/local and society/law. Laoting, Hebei, is upgrading its construction support industry cluster through group coordination to build export channels; Shuangyashan, Heilongjiang, is digitizing autumn grain purchasing to speed procedures and ensure timely payment, while leading firms extend processing chains to increase local grain value; an undergraduate team at East China University of Science and Technology spent two years adapting aerospace phase-change thermal protection for high-voltage vehicle connectors. A private survey showed China’s factory activity expanded at the slowest pace since July, with weaker overseas orders. On society and law, a Miao village in Chengbu, Hunan, cleared alleys and fire lanes and installed "electronic sentinels" on old wooden houses; after a magnitude-7.0 earthquake in Baqing, Tibet, a heavy-lift unmanned helicopter was sent to Mairu Township for rescue support and surface rupture surveys; "professional negative reviews" became a business charging thousands of yuan, with one shop nearly closing after repeated complaints.
