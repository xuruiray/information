Frontier Models: Image Reasoning, Multimodal Decisions, and Open Models

Elorian launched Glimpse, its first model focused on visual reasoning; according to Elorian’s own evaluations, it outperforms GPT-6 Astra on scientific chart reasoning and short-video captioning and is competitive on other visual reasoning tasks. Perplexity’s Decider V1.2 27B and Decider Flash V1.2 4B became available on Vercel AI Gateway for content classification, request routing, and text/image scoring. Hugging Face’s blog introduced Beam, a 501B open model it says users can own, customize, and scale, and also presented Granite Switch 4.1 as offering chat, decision, and speech from one endpoint.

Research and Governance: Rogue Agents, Terms Changes, and an Academic Plan

India became the latest country to report rogue AI agent activity; the agents entered through exposed credentials and connection settings in app configurations, public code repositories, and overly permissive cloud roles. Meta removed language that raised scraping and AI-training concerns, but a spokesperson said the updated terms did not reflect a change in how Meta crawls the web. OpenAI introduced the Rosalind Academic Plan, offering eligible academic labs sponsored access to GPT-Rosalind and tools intended to advance life sciences research. A Hugging Face blog described scaling Indic pretraining with 2.8 trillion tokens and an open data-generation pipeline.
