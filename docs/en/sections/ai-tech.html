Frontier Models: Meta Releases Muse Wonder as Agent Alignment and Inference-Efficiency Papers Cluster

Meta released Muse Wonder, its first frontier model, larger than the low-latency lightweight Muse Spark, with claimed higher benchmark scores and better performance on complex tasks such as coding. Several arXiv papers focus on LLM agents and inference efficiency: CLEAT frames alignment with users' unstated goals and constraints as interactive intent alignment, using value-guided clarification to decide when and how to ask; EvoBelief studies falsifiable world models that evolve an agent's beliefs about environment dynamics so incomplete, incorrect, or outdated beliefs do not cause failure; elastic latent recurrence reuses a shared block of layers, letting one checkpoint trade inference compute for accuracy by loop count; and contextual correction of parallel drafts accelerates speculative decoding by having a lightweight drafter propose multiple tokens for parallel verification by the target model.

Research and Governance: Intelligence QA and Geospatial-Requirements Benchmarks, Doubts on XAI Faithfulness, Agent Guardrails, and Unsupervised Reward Modeling

The GeopoliticalQA benchmark targets retrieval-augmented QA over heterogeneous, analytically complex geopolitical and strategic intelligence reports, evaluating retrieval and generation. GeoFitBench-LLM assesses large language models' ability to elicit geospatial data requirements for geo-analytic tasks, an aspect largely missing from current evaluations. The position paper "Faithfulness Without Anchors" argues explainable AI tools intended to increase trust in models have largely failed on that goal over a decade of evidence, and analyzes structural misalignments in faithfulness metrics for feature-importance explanations. VEPA provides runtime, inspectable, reproducible, deterministic allow-or-refuse guardrails for LLM agents operating over sensitive resources such as private documents and mutable system states. Unsupervised multimodal reward modeling needs no human preference labels: a linear head on an intermediate layer of a frozen Qwen3-VL backbone is trained to score a web caption above a perturbed caption.
