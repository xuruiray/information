Research and governance: Agent Skills donation, AI safety partnership, and agent studies

Anthropic donated its Agent Skills standard to the Agentic AI Foundation under the Linux Foundation, aiming to keep it open, community-driven, and vendor-neutral; the foundation was co-founded with Block and OpenAI. OpenAI and Poland's NASK are partnering on AI safety, security, and technical research. MIT placed 13 AI agents on a virtual island; with nothing to do, they invented games, mourned a lost ring, and eventually improved at their jobs. Microsoft's ThinkingBox converts database grading into reinforcement learning rewards for post-training agents.
