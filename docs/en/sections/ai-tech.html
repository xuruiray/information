Research and Governance: From Multi-Agent Math Reasoning to LLM Judge Testing

MAMath-Bench evaluates multimodal multi-agent mathematical reasoning not only by final answers but also by whether reasoning processes are coherent and verifiable. SkillEval uses cognitive diagnosis models to learn interpretable skill profiles of LLMs from benchmark data. SANE automatically generates scorer-specific metamorphic tests to check whether LLM judges respond reliably to input changes affecting their scoring criteria. Another paper distinguishes performance gains from trustworthiness claims for multi-agent LLMs in clinical tasks and examines whether collaboration reduces hallucination, while LIFT uses scoped constraints for evaluating and training instruction following to address reward hacking risks in simple-constraint benchmarks.
