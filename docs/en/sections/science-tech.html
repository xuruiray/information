Scientific Research: High-Energy X-Rays, Ice-Age Ocean Chain Reaction and Immune Sensing

The four studies span physics, Earth science and immunology. A new study proposed that Northern Hemisphere ice-sheet expansion cooled Atlantic waters that traveled south toward Antarctica, possibly forming an ocean chain reaction that affects ice ages. Another study identified a small group of brain cells that prevents dangerous blood pressure swings and revealed cardiovascular risk even when average readings look normal.
