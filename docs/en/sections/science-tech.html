Science & Tech: AI Chip Shares Slip, a Moon-Like Rock on Earth, and Advances Across Research

This Science & Tech section covers chips, space and scientific research. In chips, Nvidia and AMD unveiled hardware and software designs for future AI data centers, but an analyst said the revenue potential is still far off and both stocks fell. In space, an International Space Station astronaut photographed an anorthosite formation in Madagascar resembling the Moon's bright highlands; it dates back at least 600 million years and bears traces of ancient continental collisions. In research, scientists found vanillin can fight infections, reduce inflammation and promote wound healing; a study suggests the universe's fundamental constants may be finely balanced for life; an experimental injection destroyed fat cells and substantially reduced dangerous belly fat, potentially helping prevent weight regain after stopping Ozempic and Wegovy; and researchers identified a hidden factor in chemical reactions, using acidity control near electrodes to raise the desired product share from 16% to 97%.

Research advances: vanillin's healing potential, constants for life, a fat-cell injection and a hidden factor in reactions

Several research advances emerged. Scientists found vanillin can fight infections, reduce inflammation and promote wound healing, potentially aiding advanced wound treatments and regenerative medicine. Researchers report the universe's fundamental constants may be finely balanced for life, with tiny changes making liquids too thick or thin for essential biological processes. An experimental injection destroyed fat cells and substantially reduced dangerous belly fat, with early research suggesting it may help prevent weight regain after stopping Ozempic and Wegovy. Another study identified a hidden factor determining whether chemical reactions succeed, using acidity control near electrodes to raise the desired product share from 16% to 97% and proposing chemicals production from oxygen and electricity.
