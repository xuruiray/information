World Affairs Briefing: Ukraine War Losses, Gaza Strike and Political and Social Developments

This edition of World Affairs covers security, elections and rights developments: leaked data reportedly show nearly 500,000 deaths in Russia from the war in Ukraine; an Israeli air strike killed Hamas’s internal security chief in Gaza; and Ukraine confirmed naval drone strikes on Russian ships off Libya. The UK warned companies of potential Russian sabotage, while a wave of AI fake videos targeting DPP figures in Taiwan raised questions about Chinese involvement. The Gambia held an election; Kilmar Ábrego García, central to a Trump deportation dispute, left the US for Spain; and Indian political figure Saurav Das said he was barred from a UK flight. Cuba’s life expectancy fell for the first time since 1950, Congolese residents sued over a Chinese-owned copper-cobalt mine, North Korean defectors described persecution for Christian faith, and a supermarket hostage siege unfolded in Cartagena, Colombia.
