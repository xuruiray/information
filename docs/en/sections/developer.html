Development & Programming: Rvoip, datasette-charts, and sqlite3_rsync

This section covers three developer project updates: Rvoip is a VoIP stack written in Rust whose Hacker News entry received 121 points and 25 comments; datasette-charts 0.3a0 adds chart annotations—lines, shaded areas, labels, or combinations—that can be adjusted in the chart builder in Datasette Lite; sqlite3_rsync is SQLite's remote-copy tool, added as an experimental feature in 3.47.0 in 2024 and enhanced in 3.50.0 in 2025, and can be used to obtain database snapshots.
