Lead Story
Scientists may have found a shortcut to fusion energy

Princeton Plasma Physics Laboratory may have found a dramatically more efficient route to fusion energy by reversing the traditional approach: heating plasma first, then increasing its density. Their calculations suggest future reactors could reach self-sustaining fusion with far less energy while avoiding hidden problems that make ignition harder. The team says this counterintuitive sequence could help future reactors achieve ignition more easily.

CLEAT: Learning Value-Guided Clarification for Interactive Agents The paper studies interactive intent alignment through clarification, where agents must act under goals and constraints users may leave unstated. It examines when clarification is useful and how an agent can turn answers into improved actions, framing clarification as value-guided learning for interactive agents. arXiv 人工智能 SpireBench: Evaluating Long-Horizon Strategic Play of Agentic Models with Slay the Spire Most agentic benchmarks are short and episodic, leaving long-horizon strategic reasoning under-studied. SpireBench has models play the full game Slay the Spire, aiming to dissect performance into stored knowledge, reasoning, and luck over extended tasks. arXiv 人工智能 EvoBelief: Falsifiable World Models for Self-Improving LLM Agents Self-evolving agents often improve acting through experience, reflection, or skills, yet can fail when their beliefs about environment dynamics are incomplete or outdated. EvoBelief studies evolving those beliefs as a complementary axis of self-evolution and emphasizes falsifiability. arXiv 人工智能
