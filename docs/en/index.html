03 Science & Technology

This edition covers a silicon qubit advance, Solar System and astrophysics findings, and research on superconductors, language meaning, mirror-life detection and pancreatic cancer. Researchers operated five silicon qubits simultaneously at about 99.9% accuracy; Bennu samples indicated mixing of inner and outer Solar System material; a balloon telescope found thread-like magnetic structures above the Sun; three feeding black holes were found in one early galaxy; and astronomers reported a first candidate exomoon. Separate studies revealed hidden crystal patches in a long-studied superconductor, showed word meaning is organized around power, danger and order as well as emotion, proposed a method to detect hypothetical mirror life, and found pancreatic cancer cells reshape DNA to evade treatment.
